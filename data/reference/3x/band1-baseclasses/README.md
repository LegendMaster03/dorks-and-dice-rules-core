# Historical D&D 3.x SRD sources — Band 1 base-class comparison

**Purpose:** Historical source material for reviewing Dorks & Dice base classes at levels **1–20**. This is reference material, **not adopted native rules**. Base-class review excludes subclasses, prestige classes, and Band 2 progression.

## 3.5e source (locally retained)

- `srd35/classes-i.md` — Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk (and their class-related mechanics).
- `srd35/classes-ii.md` — Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Wizard, general class and multiclass rules.
- `srd35/legal.md` — retained SRD legal information and OGL 1.0a license, including copyright notices.

These Markdown documents are formatted versions of Wizards of the Coast's Revised (v3.5) SRD RTF files, transcribed and formatted in `katekorsaro/dnd3.5e-srd` (`04.done/ClassesI.md`, `04.done/ClassesII.md`, `02.conversion/Legal.md`). Because a conversion can contain formatting or transcription errors, consult the preserved original RTF archive for disputed language or values:

- Original RTF files: https://archive.org/details/dnd35srd
- Markdown conversion: https://github.com/katekorsaro/dnd3.5e-srd
- Additional RTF mirror: https://carcer.co.uk/35srd/

**License:** OGL 1.0a; the included `srd35/legal.md` contains the SRD license and Section 15 copyright notice. Do not merge the 3.5 SRD source text into CC BY 4.0 SRD source files or assume their licenses are interchangeable. Native design and source-content licensing remain separate decisions.

## Original 3.0 source (cross-check; not yet locally imported)

A working 3.0 SRD mirror is available at https://www.dragon.ee/30srd/. The Barbarian class page at https://www.dragon.ee/30srd/barbarian.htm was inspected directly. It differs mechanically from the 3.5 Barbarian, including Uncanny Dodge, damage reduction, and Rage advancement thresholds. Not every original 3.0 class file has been independently verified or locally copied. **Do not mark 3.0 source coverage complete** on the strength of the one checked page.

The approved workflow permits proceeding from 3.5e, 5e and 5.5e when 3.0 coverage is absent or uncertain; add verified 3.0 differences as evidence when useful. The original 3.0 core SRD did not include the later Warlock class.

## Existing 5.x references

- `data/reference/srd/structured/class/class-*.json` — SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2.1 base-class and class-feature candidates.
- `data/native/classes/base/*.json` and `data/native/features/classes/` — **pending** native reconciliations; do not treat unreviewed candidates as approved Dorks & Dice rules.
- `docs/rewrite/native-rules-design-specification.md` — approved mechanics, Level Band 1 sequence and reconciliation policy.

For each base class, compare exact level grants, math, resources, timing, restrictions, and interactions, and present only actual unresolved differences after applying established Dorks & Dice rules. **No edition has default precedence for player classes.** Do not pull subclass or prestige mechanics into this pass.
