# Authoritative native rules

Only selected, coherent, source-neutral Dorks & Dice rules belong here. A reviewed data-only rule is authoritative even before its C# executor exists. Pending integration does not approve unresolved dependent mechanics.

Selected Species and Ancestry definitions are in `species/`; approved independent cultural Upbringings are in `upbringings/`; Backgrounds are in `backgrounds/`. Files under `working/` are not a fallback for any runtime rule.

**Pillar 2 dependency:** The reviewed Pillar 1 Species/Ancestry parent links, defaults, selection fields, and current direct-choice examples are provisional content organization, **not a finalized Origin schema or executable inheritance system**. Pillar 2 will establish origination, mixed Ancestry, contributed heritage, and manifested-trait semantics; these may materially change the native structures. Do not implement character acquisition, Species/Ancestry selection, inheritance projection, or persisted character state based on this temporary layout. Background and Upbringing content can be reviewed separately, without assuming final Pillar 1 integration.

Species/Ancestry and Upbringing parentage use explicit stable IDs, not folder structure. A Species or Ancestry may suggest one `defaultUpbringing` stable ID; the nearest default in its selected Ancestry chain wins unless the player explicitly selects another. An Upbringing's parent chain contributes inherited benefits once but is not an additional selected Upbringing. Defaults do not impose Species restrictions. The approved data-only selection contract is `rules/species-subspecies-upbringing.json` (historical filename retained for references).

Not all Species require an Ancestry selection. Some, such as Dragonborn, require a specific playable leaf. Dragonborn Chromatic, Metallic and Gem families have five ordinary selected-native leaves apiece. All three family nodes remain nonselectable. The Gem family defines the 15-foot cone, one-way Psionic Mind and level-5 temporary Gem Flight with hover; its leaves define Force/Radiant/Psychic/Thunder/Necrotic affinities. All reuse the Dragonborn Species' ordinary breath and 50% resistance. Exceptional Platinum, Fivefold Chromatic and Ruby do not enter ordinary selection or mixed-Heritage candidate pools by virtue of this content. Cross-cultural Upbringings remain permissible. General Elf and General Gnome are explicitly **inheritance-only**, not directly selectable; their respective Ancestries suggest valid High/Wood/Drow and Forest/Rock cultural children, which inherit the general benefits once. Base Elf and Gnome Species have no `defaultUpbringing` because their former suggestions referred to now-nonselectable parent records.

The source SRD reference material stays in `data/reference/`, independent of authoritative native content. `data/reference/srd/native-coverage-manifest.json` records individual approved migrations. Provisional balance values within coherent native rules remain explicitly identified for later playtest adjustment.
