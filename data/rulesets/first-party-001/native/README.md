# Authoritative native rules

Only selected, coherent, source-neutral Dorks & Dice rules belong here. A reviewed data-only rule is authoritative even before its C# executor exists. Pending integration does not approve unresolved dependent mechanics.

**Biological gene normalization (November 2026):** Each named inherent trait has a stable independent `origin-trait` entity under `origination/biological/genes/`, with its complete original mechanical payload in `trait`. The 85 active canonical biological genes each occupy a root-level file, identified by their mechanical meaning, without Species/Ancestry prefixes. Four pairs (Natural Climber, Low-Light Vision, Darkness, and Powerful Build) have unified definitions. Powerful Build grants both the Grappled escape advantage and enhanced carrying, pushing, dragging and lifting to both Species; native innate spells have no prepared-spell mode. Species/Ancestry records store ordered `{"traitId": "trait.…"}` references. The native catalog resolves references into a read-only embedded-style projection for existing API and mechanics consumers; it separately exposes canonical trait entities and their IDs. The ordering and source occurrence paths (`/traits/N`) remain unchanged. The biological gene directory classifies eligible building blocks; actual manifestation remains player-selected and subject to authored relationships, dependencies, reconciliation, and authority. Explicitly reviewed Amphibious, Natural Climber, and Low-Light Vision occurrences share canonical definitions when their mechanics agree; shared names alone do not establish mechanical equivalence. Intrinsic creature type, size, movement and spellcasting-ability choice fields remain on Species/Ancestry. These references do not authorize stacking, transmission, or any new Origination outcome. Persisted profiles created using the previous canonical gene IDs require explicit migration with `tools/migrate_biological_gene_ids.py` and `data/migrations/biological-gene-ids-20261121.json`; old IDs do not remain active catalog aliases. Identity migration does not update already-saved mechanical grant payloads: profiles affected by Powerful Build's unified effects or obsolete prepared-spell properties need verified reconstruction before gameplay. See `docs/rewrite/character-origins/origin-trait-normalization-20261114.md`.

Selected Species and Ancestry presets are in `origination/biological/species/`; approved independent cultural Upbringings are in `upbringings/`; Backgrounds are in `backgrounds/`. Files under `working/` are not a fallback for any runtime rule.

**Pillar 2:** Species/Ancestry presets and biological genes are consumed by the same universal Origin construction pipeline. Source eligibility, player-selected manifestation, dependent choices, and reconstruction are partly executable; biological relationships and complete native mechanical projection are still being developed. Do not treat directory organization as authority to grant an unmanifested characteristic. Background and Upbringing content can be reviewed separately, without assuming final Pillar 1 integration.

Species/Ancestry and Upbringing parentage use explicit stable IDs, not folder structure. A Species or Ancestry may suggest one `defaultUpbringing` stable ID; the nearest default in its selected Ancestry chain wins unless the player explicitly selects another. An Upbringing's parent chain contributes inherited benefits once but is not an additional selected Upbringing. Defaults do not impose Species restrictions. The approved data-only selection contract is `rules/species-subspecies-upbringing.json` (historical filename retained for references).

Not all Species require an Ancestry selection. Some, such as Dragonborn, require a specific playable leaf. Dragonborn Chromatic, Metallic and Gem families retain five ordinary **selected-native** leaf definitions apiece. **Chromatic and Metallic leaves are selectable; the Gem family and all five ordinary Gem leaves are temporarily `selectable: false` until psionics is revisited separately.** Their authored records remain authoritative **reference data**, including the Gem family's 15-foot cone, Psionic Mind and level-5 temporary hovering Gem Flight and its leaves' Force/Radiant/Psychic/Thunder/Necrotic affinities. Gem ancestry must not be offered for ordinary character creation or ordinary mixed-Heritage contributor selection during this deferral. This selection restriction does not delete or rebalance those traits, make them a fallback source, or add eligibility to exceptional Ruby. Exceptional Platinum, Fivefold Chromatic and Ruby do not enter ordinary selection or mixed-Heritage candidate pools by virtue of this content. Cross-cultural Upbringings remain permissible. General Elf and General Gnome are explicitly **inheritance-only**, not directly selectable; their respective Ancestries suggest valid High/Wood/Drow and Forest/Rock cultural children, which inherit the general benefits once. Base Elf and Gnome Species have no `defaultUpbringing` because their former suggestions referred to now-nonselectable parent records.

The source SRD reference material stays in `data/reference/`, independent of authoritative native content. `data/reference/srd/native-coverage-manifest.json` records individual approved migrations. Provisional balance values within coherent native rules remain explicitly identified for later playtest adjustment.
