# Bigby — six Goliath Subspecies expansion candidates

**Status:** historical candidate research and rejected alternatives for the current first pass. The six SRD 5.2.1 boons are native, augmented with merged level-1 Strike effects and level-4 advanced non-ASI features (see `GOLIATH_STRIKE_OVERLAP_REVIEW.md`). The earlier minor passive additions proposed here — Mist Sight, Heat-Hardened, Winter Strider, Steady Footing, Rock Concealment and Amphibious — are **not** granted in addition.

**New overlap findings:** [`GOLIATH_STRIKE_OVERLAP_REVIEW.md`](GOLIATH_STRIKE_OVERLAP_REVIEW.md) compares the six original Strike options against the current SRD 5.2.1 Goliath boons. Fire Strike is a mechanical duplicate; Frost and Hill substantially overlap. It evaluates an early Strike grant and level-4 advanced ancestry progression, rather than assuming the previous provisional level-9 recommendation.

**Source boundary:** *Bigby Presents: Glory of the Giants* (2023) contains giantkind lore, character feats, two backgrounds, giant-themed monsters and the Ordning. The book **does not define six Goliath Subspecies**. The following secondary Goliath adaptations are newly proposed design applications of the giant types; they must not be misidentified as published Goliath Species traits. Source-backed giant lore is distinguished from the proposed mechanical adaptation below.

### Source-specific distinctions

- Official *Bigby's* publisher material identifies the six giant kinds and the eight-feat suite; its character options are **feats/backgrounds**, not an existing Goliath ancestry progression. The six associated follow-up feats are *Guile of the Cloud Giant*, *Ember of the Fire Giant*, *Fury of the Frost Giant*, *Vigor of the Hill Giant*, *Keenness of the Stone Giant* and *Soul of the Storm Giant*.
- **Cloud:** giant affinity for mist and fog is supported by the publisher's introduction. *Guile of the Cloud Giant* offers a more potent reaction-based escape/mitigation, not a reason to give every Cloud Goliath that feat.
- **Fire/Frost:** *Ember* and *Fury* both provide elemental resistance plus advanced combat powers. A two-type elemental-resistance package is a coherent **alternative** to heat/snow utility, but would increase raw defensive effectiveness, duplicate future feat features and potentially overshadow the other four ancestry leaves. Do not grant it selectively without comparing all six.
- **Hill:** *Vigor* contains force-movement/Prone protection and food-related recovery. Physical balance rather than stereotyped intelligence or compulsory gluttony is the defensible innate expression. Meal traditions belong to Upbringing.
- **Stone:** *Keenness* includes darkvision and an enhanced magical rock attack. Darkvision could be a simpler alternative to Rock Concealment but needs comparison with the Dwarf and other Species senses. Art and stoneworking are learned Competencies and not automatically inherited.
- **Storm:** the official 2014 and 2024 Storm Giant stat blocks confirm Amphibious; this is a stronger anatomical model for a secondary trait than automatic rune magic, prophecy, or the *Soul* feat's temporary storm aura.
- **Class/feat boundaries:** Path of the Giant Barbarian and Bigby's follow-up feats are potential class or optional feat content; granting their full packages as Species traits would override their independent costs and produce untested power stacking.

## Alternative expansion models to evaluate

**Model A — modest secondary innate utility (recommended):** the six bounded traits in the table. Each retains the existing ancestry boon and gains a contextual adaptation. The value is mostly exploration or defensive utility, comparable to the individual Dragonborn innate adaptations. This is a testable first-pass package, not a final balance solution.

**Model B — elemental defenses for all six:** Fire and Frost receive 50% resistance; Storm receives Lightning/Thunder resistance (choose explicit type or limited mode); Stone receives physical damage reduction or protection; Cloud receives reaction-based mitigation; Hill receives physical protection. This model demands a substantially more detailed numerical balance comparison and interacts with Dorks & Dice's percentage-based resistance model, existing reaction usage, elemental hazard rules and selected race-specific defenses. It is **not** recommended as an automatic bundle.

**Model C — ancestry magic progression:** grant watered-down versions of each *Bigby's* follow-up feat at a later character level. This is the least attractive initial option: it risks consuming design space intended for feats and adds active powers on top of already strong PB/LR boons and the shared level-5 Large Form.

## Relative balance and integration risks

1. **Do not rank powers by the Ordning.** The Ordning is a social tradition, not a mandate to make Storm numerically superior to Hill. Individual Goliaths need not follow its ideology.
2. **Do not automatically tie learned traditions to biological ancestry.** Giant Foundling, Rune Carver, runesmithing, religion, culture, languages and class training remain Upbringing/Background/Competency/Feat matters, even if associated by default later.
3. **Do not grant the complete ancestry feats.** Strike of the Giants can be chosen independently; otherwise native SRD boon + equivalent feat attacks could stack. Revisit stack and eligibility rules at the dedicated feat pass.
4. **Check native-condition semantics.** Hill's Tumble uses native Prone (−4 AC against melee), so Hill already has a potentially disproportionate offensive boon; Steady Footing is deliberately limited. Rock Concealment must require ordinary concealment; it cannot allow hiding in open sight.
5. **Respect runtime boundary.** The selected environmental hazard records and skill procedures are not all executable in the native character evaluator; document capabilities in native only after selection, but do not manufacture finished mechanics-engine support.
6. **Separate source and proposal.** The specific Mist Sight, Heat-Hardened, Winter Strider, Steady Footing and Rock Concealment text is **our proposed adaptation**, not a published Bigby's player-character feature. Amphibious is directly modeled on published Storm Giant creature mechanics.

## Later-level giant ancestry features — earlier level-9 proposal, superseded by selected level 4

**Owner direction under discussion:** Instead of making the six *Bigby's* higher-tier giant feats into Origin feats, consider **granting their supernatural/non-ASI benefits automatically through the corresponding Goliath Subspecies at a later character level**. This supersedes the earlier preference for only small passive secondary adaptations *as the leading option to assess*, but is not yet a confirmed native rule. Existing six SRD 5.2.1 boons are retained.

**Earlier progression candidate (superseded for investigation):** ancestry boon at level 1; shared Large Form at level 5; an advanced ancestry feature at **character level 9**. The more recent owner direction evaluates advanced features around **level 4**, with possible early Strike grants. See the separate overlap review before selecting levels. Consider level 11 if tier-2 burst is excessive. Do not simply use the original feat's level-4 prerequisite as the Species feature grant level. The resulting ability is an inherited Subspecies feature, **not** a feat selection and **not** a bonus Origin feat.

**Prerequisites:** Published advanced feats require level 4 plus a matching Strike of the Giants option. **Inherited Subspecies features should not silently grant Strike of the Giants.** Granting an adapted advanced feature by ancestry at level 9 would be a deliberate exception to that prerequisite chain. Independently selected feats, if later available, must not result in duplicate identical benefits or pooled resource tracking without an explicit stacking rule.

**Ability scores and save DCs:** All six original advanced feats include a +1 ability-score improvement. If the abilities are inherited rather than earned as feats, remove that generic improvement. Where feat text keys its attack/save DC to the increased score (especially Stone and Storm), use a separately reconciled source such as Constitution or a stated player choice; **do not embed an invalid dangling reference to a removed feat ASI**.

**Level timing:** At level 5, shared Large Form already unlocks, adding 10 feet of Speed and Advantage on Strength checks with temporary Large size. The previous level-9 or level-11 spacing suggestion is retained as historical design context, but the current proposal to investigate grants advanced features around **level 4**. That means a level-4 advanced power immediately precedes Large Form at 5; playtest the combined power concentration. Evaluate the strength of all six against other Species, not just against each other.

**Supersession of earlier passive suggestions:** The bounded Mist Sight, Heat-Hardened, Winter Strider, Steady Footing, Rock Concealment and Amphibious proposals are **alternatives**, not assumed extra benefits **on top of** the advanced features. In particular, Fire's elemental defenses, Hill's Bulwark, Stone's Darkvision and Storm's aura could make the earlier additions redundant or push the package too far.

**Open decisions before native promotion:** choose grant level; decide full supernatural feat packages versus adapted slices; choose governing ability for Stone/Storm DCs; confirm that generic +1 ASIs and feat-chain prerequisites are omitted; define conflict handling if a player can later separately select a corresponding feat. No native Subspecies records are changed in this proposal.
