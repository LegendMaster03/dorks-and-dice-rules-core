# Gem Dragon hoard themes — *Fizban's* reference (deferred)

**Gem Dragonborn are outside the current Chromatic/Metallic first pass.** The five entries below are research summaries, **not** approved Gem Subspecies, powers, Upbringings, or character choices.

| Gem Dragon | *Fizban's* favored-hoard themes (paraphrased) | Potential future learned tradition, NOT published |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amethyst | Crystals and gems; scholarly writings, instruments and objects from distant cultures or planes | Scholarship, planar history, unusual instruments |
| Crystal | Sparkling art, skies-and-stars imagery, divination tools and gems chosen for beauty | Astronomy, divination lore, gemwork |
| Emerald | Unusual coins, numismatic distinctions and historically important artifacts | Numismatics, historical provenance, archive work |
| Sapphire | Battlefield relics, arms and armor, military histories and precisely cataloged trophies | Military history, cataloging, equipment identification |
| Topaz | Gold and yellow-orange gems; maritime imagery, sunlight objects and mirrors | Navigation, maritime lore, optical craft |
