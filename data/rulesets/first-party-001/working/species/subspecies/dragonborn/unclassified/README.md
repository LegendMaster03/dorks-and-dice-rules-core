# Unclassified and transformation-related dragon research (deferred)

This existing research directory collects dragon varieties that do not yet have an approved stable relationship to the Chromatic, Metallic or Gem Dragonborn hierarchies.

- **Time Dragons:** additional source/taxonomy comparison still required.
- **Deep Dragons:** [hoard preferences and possible learned traditions](DEEP_DRAGON_HOARD_RESEARCH.md).
- **Moonstone Dragons:** [hoard preferences and possible learned traditions](MOONSTONE_DRAGON_HOARD_RESEARCH.md).
- **Shadow Dragons:** [hoard preferences and possible learned traditions](SHADOW_DRAGON_HOARD_RESEARCH.md); often a transformed dragon, not necessarily an inheritable lineage.
- **Faerie Dragons:** [hoard preferences and possible learned traditions](FAERIE_DRAGON_HOARD_RESEARCH.md); distinct dragon creature, no ordinary Dragonborn lineage approved.
- **Dragon Turtles:** [hoard preferences and possible learned traditions](DRAGON_TURTLE_HOARD_RESEARCH.md); separate aquatic draconic creature, no ordinary Dragonborn lineage approved.

The five linked hoard studies are based on *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5, Draconomicon. They complement the ten current Chromatic/Metallic hoard entries and five deferred Gem entries in [the source index](../HOARD_SOURCE_INDEX.md).

Only research belongs here. No new playable Subspecies, innate features, cultural Upbringings, defaults, or mechanics are approved by these notes.
