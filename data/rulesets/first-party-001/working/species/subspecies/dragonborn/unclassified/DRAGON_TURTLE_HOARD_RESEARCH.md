# Dragon Turtle — hoard and cultural-tradition research

**Status:** deferred source research only. This is not an approved Species, Subspecies, Upbringing, or native rule.
**Source:** *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5: Draconomicon, **Dragon Turtle Treasures** subsection. Publisher's source index: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ftod.

**Classification:** a separate aquatic draconic creature, not one of the fifteen standard Dragonborn Subspecies and not necessarily a suitable humanoid lineage.

## What the published source supports
- Their hoards can include pearls, coral, and goods given in tribute by underwater peoples, alongside coin, trade metals, and objects recovered from shipwrecks.
- Surface-crafted possessions—especially ones made of durable materials such as stone or metal—can be especially prized in an aquatic environment.
- Collections can combine goods from many places, and some individuals investigate the provenance or function of unusual surface-world objects.

## Candidate learned Upbringing applications — Dorks & Dice proposals
- A **maritime salvage and surface-artifact tradition** could cover identifying submerged cargo, recording wreck locations, appraising unfamiliar imports, and preserving water-sensitive finds.
- Possible existing mechanical references: `skill.history`, `skill.appraise`, `skill.survival`, `competency.cartography`; confirm aquatic-specific qualification with the skill/competency system before choosing exact benefits.
- Breathing underwater, a swim speed, or a protective shell would require independent Species/Subspecies mechanics; hoard knowledge alone grants none of them.

## Boundary and questions
- A Dragon Turtle-inspired cultural tradition need not imply ancestry, ritual tribute, raiding ships, or compulsory treasure gathering.
- Determine whether any future default Upbringing would be associated with an approved Species/Subspecies identity; default references belong on Species/Subspecies JSON, never as reverse lists.
- Compare the chosen Skill/Competency grants against the same training budget as the ten Chromatic and Metallic hoard-inspired traditions.
- Keep any future biology or magical lineage choices separate from cultural benefits. No approved rules or character options are created by this file.
