# Moonstone Dragon — hoard and cultural-tradition research

**Status:** deferred source research only. This is not an approved Species, Subspecies, Upbringing, or native rule.
**Source:** *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5: Draconomicon, **Moonstone Dragon Treasures** subsection. Publisher's source index: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ftod.

**Classification:** a distinct 5e dragon profile; determine its relationship to Gem, Fey and other taxonomies during a future pass. Do not silently classify it as a standard Gem Dragonborn type.

## What the published source supports
- These dragons often favor silver, platinum, mithral, and pale or silvery gems rather than copper, gold, or more colorful stones.
- They also collect playful, strange, beautiful, or fanciful art and objects with unusual design or a connection to fey aesthetics.
- Material and artistic preferences vary among individuals; the source does not imply every Moonstone Dragon rejects the same metals.

## Candidate learned Upbringing applications — Dorks & Dice proposals
- A **fey-art and silversmithing tradition** might emphasize recognizing fey-themed artwork, precious metals, decorative work, and the cultural context of unusual relics.
- Possible existing mechanical references: `skill.arcana`, `skill.appraise`, `skill.history`, `competency.gemcutting`, `competency.smithing`; define a comparable benefit budget later.
- Fey magical effects and innate dragon powers are not interchangeable with learned Upbringing features.

## Boundary and questions
