# Shadow Dragon — hoard and cultural-tradition research

**Status:** deferred source research only. This is not an approved Species, Subspecies, Upbringing, or native rule.
**Source:** *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5: Draconomicon, **Shadow Dragon Treasures** subsection. Publisher's source index: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ftod.

**Classification:** often a dragon altered by Shadowfell influence. This is a transformation-related source profile, not automatically a heritable Shadow Dragonborn Subspecies.

## What the published source supports
- A Shadow Dragon can retain the original dragon type's treasure preferences; the older basis of the hoard still matters.
- Over time, brilliant objects may become unpleasant to it, leading to dulled or tarnished finishes and objects kept out of sight.
- Elaborate hidden caches, concealed art, dim or worn valuables, and secret storage can feature in these hoards.

## Candidate learned Upbringing applications — Dorks & Dice proposals
- A **hidden-archive or artifact-restoration tradition** might train cataloging concealed collections, examining damaged art, recovering provenance, and careful storage.
- Possible existing mechanical references: `skill.investigation`, `skill.search`, `skill.history`, `skill.appraise`, plus an approved relevant craft Competency.
- Any ability to perceive in darkness, transform into shadow, or impose supernatural corruption is a separate creature/mechanics question, not an Upbringing inferred from hoard lore.

## Boundary and questions
- Do not flatten Shadow Dragon treasure preference to an elemental ancestry or imply that all Shadow Dragons simply prefer onyx; their earlier dragon type remains consequential.
- Determine whether any future default Upbringing would be associated with an approved Species/Subspecies identity; default references belong on Species/Subspecies JSON, never as reverse lists.
- Compare the chosen Skill/Competency grants against the same training budget as the ten Chromatic and Metallic hoard-inspired traditions.
- Keep any future biology or magical lineage choices separate from cultural benefits. No approved rules or character options are created by this file.
