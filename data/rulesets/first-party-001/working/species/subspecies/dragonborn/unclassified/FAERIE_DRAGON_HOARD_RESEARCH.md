# Faerie Dragon — hoard and cultural-tradition research

**Status:** deferred source research only. This is not an approved Species, Subspecies, Upbringing, or native rule.
**Source:** *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5: Draconomicon, **Faerie Dragon Treasures** subsection. Publisher's source index: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ftod.

**Classification:** a distinct small draconic creature in the 5e Draconomicon, not established as an inheritable standard Dragonborn Subspecies.

## What the published source supports
- Faerie dragon treasures are mixtures of genuine valuables and seemingly trivial curiosities, trinkets, toys, and unusual found objects.
- The stories told about objects can matter more than their monetary worth; a modest item may be cherished for an imaginative or surprising association.
- The example objects suggest puzzles, disguises, humorous performances, and fantastical miniatures without establishing any mandatory hoarding behavior.

## Candidate learned Upbringing applications — Dorks & Dice proposals
- A **curiosity-and-storycraft tradition** might focus on riddles, storytelling, art-object interpretation, puzzles, and the study of unusual possessions.
- Possible existing mechanical references: `skill.investigation`, `skill.deception`, `skill.history`, or another approved artistic Competency after reviewing the catalog. These are proposals, not imported source features.
- Do not automatically grant Faerie Dragon innate spellcasting, flight, invisibility, or other creature powers as part of an Upbringing.

## Boundary and questions
- The source describes how some Faerie Dragons value objects, not how all people raised around them must behave. It does not establish an automatically selectable Faerie Dragonborn lineage.
- Determine whether any future default Upbringing would be associated with an approved Species/Subspecies identity; default references belong on Species/Subspecies JSON, never as reverse lists.
- Compare the chosen Skill/Competency grants against the same training budget as the ten Chromatic and Metallic hoard-inspired traditions.
- Keep any future biology or magical lineage choices separate from cultural benefits. No approved rules or character options are created by this file.
