# Deep Dragon — hoard and cultural-tradition research

**Status:** deferred source research only. This is not an approved Species, Subspecies, Upbringing, or native rule.
**Source:** *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 5: Draconomicon, **Deep Dragon Treasures** subsection. Publisher's source index: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ftod.

**Classification:** a separate 5e dragon profile, outside the fifteen standard Chromatic/Metallic/Gem Dragonborn types. Future classification remains to be reconciled.

## What the published source supports
- These dragons especially prize objects from distant or otherwise hard-to-reach places and keepsakes linked to remarkable hunts or expeditions.
- They favor dark-hued or iridescent gemstones and elaborate crafted displays, organizing treasures for examination rather than merely piling them together.
- Provenance, distinctiveness, and recounting an object's journey matter alongside material value.

## Candidate learned Upbringing applications — Dorks & Dice proposals
- An **expedition-curation tradition** could train investigation of an object's origin, preparation of travel collections, and documenting a journey.
- Possible existing mechanical references for a future Upbringing: `skill.history`, `skill.appraise`, `skill.survival`, `competency.gemcutting`, `competency.cartography`—choose a balanced subset, not all.
- An innate dragon-type adaptation, if later considered, requires separate biological/magical source comparison; a hoard preference does not establish one.

## Boundary and questions
- Do not assume that Dragonborn descended from a Deep Dragon necessarily travel, hoard objects, or collect rare gems.
- Determine whether any future default Upbringing would be associated with an approved Species/Subspecies identity; default references belong on Species/Subspecies JSON, never as reverse lists.
- Compare the chosen Skill/Competency grants against the same training budget as the ten Chromatic and Metallic hoard-inspired traditions.
- Keep any future biology or magical lineage choices separate from cultural benefits. No approved rules or character options are created by this file.
