# Dragonborn shared mechanics — 5e/5.5e comparison and unresolved source evidence

The selected common Dragonborn Species mechanics are authoritative in `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`. This note preserves historical differences and unresolved decisions rather than supplying alternative executable rules. The pinned SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2.1 source texts remain at `data/reference/srd/`.

## Approved shared framework
- Humanoid, Medium, 30-foot walking speed, provisional 60-foot Darkvision.
- 50 percent resistance to the element associated with the selected dragon-type Subspecies.
- Breath Weapon replaces one attack during the Attack action on the Dragonborn's turn.
- Uses equal Proficiency Bonus, replenished on Long Rest.
- Dexterity save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Constitution modifier; success takes half damage.
- Damage 1d10 at level 1, 2d10 at level 5, 3d10 at level 11, 4d10 at level 17.
- Family/individual type supplies breath damage type and geometry. **Chromatic has a selected 30-foot, 5-foot-wide line**, while Metallic geometry remains unresolved.

## Relevant published alternatives — not selected
- **5e SRD 5.1:** ancestry-specific cone/line, sometimes Constitution rather than Dexterity saves; Action activation; 2d6 at level 1, increasing to 5d6 at level 16; one use per Short or Long Rest; no Darkvision granted by the Species.
- **5e Fizban's Treasury of Dragons:** differentiated Chromatic, Metallic, Gem Dragonborn; attack-replacement breath and Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest. Chromatic Warding, Metallic alternative breath, and Gem-specific abilities distinguish families.
- **5.5e SRD 5.2.1:** permits cone or line chosen at use and adds level-5 temporary flight for general Dragonborn. Geometry and flight are not assumed for this custom ruleset.
- **3.5e Races of the Dragon (Dragonborn of Bahamut):** Heart aspect has a breath that recharges after 1d4 rounds, with different damage progression and action economy. This is a transformed exceptional ancestry reference, not the selected ordinary Dragonborn resource mechanic.
- **3.5e Player's Handbook II (Dragon Shaman):** individual dragon totem adaptations are candidates for physical Subspecies abilities; breath recharge and class auras are not automatically Species traits.
- **3.5e true dragons:** metallic secondary breaths, sensory/environmental adaptations, and older Green Dragon acid breath require specific review before promotion.
