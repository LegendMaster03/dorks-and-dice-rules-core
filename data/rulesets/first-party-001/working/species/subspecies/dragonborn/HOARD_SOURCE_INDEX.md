# *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* — Draconomicon hoard source index

**Research-only source inventory.** Chapter 5 of *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021) includes a section about favored treasures and a table of art objects for **20 kinds of dragons or draconic creatures**. It covers every one of the original fifteen Chromatic, Metallic and Gem dragons, plus five additional kinds. The 20 entries are not all new Dragonborn Subspecies.

## Active Dragonborn reconciliation (10)
- **Chromatic** — Black, Blue, Green, Red, White. Hoard-to-Upbringing candidates: `chromatic/HOARD_TRADITIONS_RESEARCH.md`.
- **Metallic** — Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold, Silver. Hoard-to-Upbringing candidates: `metallic/HOARD_TRADITIONS_RESEARCH.md`.

## Deferred Dragonborn reconciliation (5)
- **Gem** — Amethyst, Crystal, Emerald, Sapphire, Topaz. Keep descriptions as research in `gem/HOARD_TRADITIONS_RESEARCH.md`; do not produce playable Gem leaves or selected Upbringing defaults in this pass.

## Other Draconomicon entries (5), not approved as Dragonborn
- Deep Dragon; Dragon Turtle; Faerie Dragon; Moonstone Dragon; Shadow Dragon.
- These are extra source profiles, not members of the fifteen standard Chromatic/Metallic/Gem Dragonborn ancestries and not necessarily all independently inherited dragon types. Shadow forms particularly require distinguishing transformed dragons from lineage.

## How to use hoard lore
1. Record the **published dragon's preference** (what it treasures, why, and any associated knowledge or craft), with a source locator to its Chapter 5 entry.
2. Separately suggest a **learned tradition** rooted in those materials or practices: history, appraisal, archives, arts, crafts, trade, navigation, etc. This suggestion is a Dorks & Dice design candidate, not published character mechanics.
3. Distinguish a culturally acquired benefit from an **innate anatomical, sensory, or magical adaptation**, which would belong in the appropriate Species/Subspecies rather than Upbringing.
4. No Species is required to hoard, favor any treasure, worship a particular dragon, or follow a particular alignment. Upbringings remain independently selectable, and any later `defaultUpbringing` pointer belongs on Species/Subspecies.
5. Evaluate numerical choices against the **same benefit budget** across options rather than copying the 3.5e Dragon Shaman's +5 Appraise/Search as a free racial ability.
