# White Dragonborn — individual Subspecies research (unresolved)

**Family:** Chromatic. **Damage type:** Cold. **Approved shared Dragonborn traits:** see `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`.

## Source comparison
- 5e SRD 5.1 traditional Breath Weapon shape: 15-foot cone.
- *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* 5e Chromatic family breath: 30-foot by 5-foot line for all five Chromatic types (proposed family default, not yet selected).
- 5.5e SRD 5.2.1: universal choice of cone or line.
- 3.5e Dragon Shaman (*Player's Handbook II*) totem adaptation: Unimpeded movement on icy terrain. This was a **class ability** and does not automatically transfer into inherited Dragonborn mechanics.

**Proposed first-pass adaptation (unapproved):** Icewalker—move and climb across icy surfaces without ordinary ability checks and ignore extra movement costs from ice/snow Difficult Terrain; plus a **15-foot Swim Speed** (no water breathing). This follows two features present on the native White Dragon Wyrmling. The staged slippery-ice hazard uses a Dexterity saving throw, so immunity to that saving throw must be explicitly decided, not inferred from the source's ability-check exemption.

For the comparative case and limits, see `CHROMATIC_BALANCE_RECONCILIATION.md`.

## Reconciliation question
Strong physical adaptation candidate; examine existing slippery-terrain, Acrobatics and movement rules.

**Not approved:** any extra innate adaptation, family geometry, Chromatic Warding or cultural Upbringing. These remain separate selection decisions. Do not make alignment, ecological preferences, or loyalty mandatory for this Dragonborn type.

Sources: SRD 5.1/5.2.1 under `data/reference/srd/`; *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021); *Player's Handbook II* (2006) Dragon Shaman.
