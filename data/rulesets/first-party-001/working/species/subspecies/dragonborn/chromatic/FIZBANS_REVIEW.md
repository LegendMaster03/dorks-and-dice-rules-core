# Chromatic Dragonborn — candidate mechanics from published editions

**Research only. Active scope:** Black, Blue, Green, Red, White.

## 5e source comparisons
- **SRD 5.1:** ancestry controls elemental damage resistance and Breath Weapon type. The original breath consumes an Action, is usable once per Short or Long Rest, deals progressive d6 damage and has ancestry-specific cone/line geometry.
- **Fizban's Treasury of Dragons (2021):** Chromatic Dragonborn share a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line breath replacing one attack within the Attack action; d10 damage increases at character levels 5/11/17 and uses equal Proficiency Bonus recharge on Long Rest. At level 5 Chromatic Warding offers temporary immunity to the ancestry's element once per Long Rest.
- **SRD 5.2.1 (5.5e):** the general Dragonborn breath framework offers cone/line flexibility and temporary Draconic Flight at level 5. Reconcile instead of automatically stacking this with *Fizban's* Warding.
- Elemental affinities: Black/acid; Blue/lightning; Green/poison; Red/fire; White/cold.

## Potential enhancements, not yet selected
- 3.5e true-dragon abilities and Dragon Shaman totem-specific environmental/sensory adaptations may supply distinctive physical Subspecies traits after separate review.
- Earlier 3.5e Green Dragons used acid breath; 5e Green Dragons use poison. Do not silently combine or replace.
- Dragon-related specialized training belongs to independent Upbringings; optional cross-ancestry abilities might later be supported by the proposed Dragon Shaman species-specific feat.
- No biological alignment, allegiance, or cultural mandate.
