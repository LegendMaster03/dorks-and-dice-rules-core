# Black Dragonborn — individual Subspecies research (unresolved)

**Family:** Chromatic. **Damage type:** Acid. **Approved shared Dragonborn traits:** see `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`.

## Source comparison
- 5e SRD 5.1 traditional Breath Weapon shape: 30-foot by 5-foot line.
- *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* 5e Chromatic family breath: 30-foot by 5-foot line for all five Chromatic types (proposed family default, not yet selected).
- 5.5e SRD 5.2.1: universal choice of cone or line.
- 3.5e Dragon Shaman (*Player's Handbook II*) totem adaptation: Underwater breathing. This was a **class ability** and does not automatically transfer into inherited Dragonborn mechanics.

**Proposed first-pass adaptation (unapproved):** Amphibious—breathe air and water indefinitely; no automatic Swim Speed. The native Black Dragon Wyrmling has Amphibious and a Swim Speed; only the breathing effect is proposed here.

For the comparative case and limits, see `CHROMATIC_BALANCE_RECONCILIATION.md`.

## Reconciliation question
Strong candidate for innate gill-like or aquatic adaptation; review durability against other leaves.
