# Metallic Dragonborn — candidate mechanics from published editions

**Historical source comparison only. Selected native scope:** Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold, Silver. Approved mechanics reside in `native/species/subspecies/dragonborn/metallic.json` and its five leaf records.

## 5e source comparisons
- **SRD 5.1:** ancestry controls elemental resistance and Breath Weapon type. Original breath is an Action with one use per Short/Long Rest, progressive d6 damage, and dragon-specific cone/line geometry.
- **Fizban's Treasury of Dragons (2021):** Metallic Dragonborn share a 15-foot cone for damage. Breath replaces one attack within the Attack action; d10 damage progression at character levels 1/5/11/17 and Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest. At level 5 a separately limited Metallic Breath Weapon offers an incapacitating effect or a repelling effect instead of ordinary damage.
- **SRD 5.2.1 (5.5e):** general breath geometry flexibility and temporary Draconic Flight at level 5 require comparison with the family-specific breath effects before selection.
- Elemental affinities: Brass/fire; Bronze/lightning; Copper/acid; Gold/fire; Silver/cold.

## Historical alternatives, not selected
- Metallic true dragons often have nondamaging secondary breath effects in 3.5e; examine the individual source mechanics and saving throws.
- 3.5e Dragon Shaman offers individual dragon-totem adaptations; it is a class, and its party auras/healing do not automatically become Species abilities.
- Additional historical Metallic Dragons (Steel, Mercury) are outside this first pass.
- Cultural practice is separate from ancestry; Upbringings must remain independently selectable.
