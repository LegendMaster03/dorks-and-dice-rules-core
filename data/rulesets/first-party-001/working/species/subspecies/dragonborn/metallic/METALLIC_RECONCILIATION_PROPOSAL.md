# Metallic Dragonborn — first-pass reconciliation decisions and historical research

**Status: the Metallic family and five leaf adaptations are approved and implemented in `native/species/subspecies/dragonborn/`. This document records source comparisons and balance questions; it is not an alternate runtime rule. No Upbringing or default is approved.**
**Scope:** Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold and Silver. The approved shared Species is `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`, and the selected Chromatic comparison is `native/species/subspecies/dragonborn/chromatic.json`.

## Selected Metallic family — research rationale (`subspecies.dragonborn.metallic`)

- Nonselectable intermediate Subspecies, parent `species.dragonborn`. Five selectable Metallic leaves inherit from it through explicit stable parent IDs. Do not duplicate the approved Species Breath Weapon, resistance, size, darkvision, speed, or ancestry traits.
- **Selected ordinary damaging breath:** 15-foot cone, following published *Fizban's* Metallic Dragonborn and contrasting with the selected Chromatic 30-foot by 5-foot line. Damage, use count, Dexterity save, progression and Attack replacement all remain inherited from `species.dragonborn`. SRD 5.1's individual line/cone shapes and SRD 5.2.1's flexible shapes remain reference alternatives, not additional choices.
- **Selected secondary Metallic Breath:** beginning at character level 5, once per Long Rest **independently** of the normal Proficiency Bonus Breath Weapon uses. On your turn, replace one attack in your Attack action to exhale a 15-foot cone; DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Constitution modifier. Choose one effect when used:
  - **Enervating:** Constitution save. Failure: Incapacitated until the start of your next turn; success: no effect.
  - **Repulsion:** Strength save. Failure: pushed 20 feet directly away and knocked Prone; success: no effect.
- No extra universal Draconic Flight; no additional bonus breath uses; no culture or alignment restriction.
- **Comparative playtest decision:** retain both published secondary-breath effects and their level-5 timing for the first playtest. This provides a coherent rule to test, not proof of balance against Chromatic Warding. The secondary breath is a separate resource (one shared use for either effect), not an upgrade to the ordinary Breath Weapon's Proficiency Bonus uses. Do not grant both choices as two separate uses.
- **Native-condition implications:** `condition.incapacitated` disallows Actions, Bonus Actions and Reactions, **ends Concentration**, and does not independently stop movement, speech or awareness. The end of the breath's condition does not restore lost Concentration. A Grapple can also end under the native Grappled condition if its grappler is Incapacitated. `condition.prone` imposes −4 AC against melee attacks and +4 AC against ranged attacks, imposes Disadvantage on the affected creature's own attacks, and costs half Speed to stand; do **not** substitute ordinary published 5e attacker Advantage/Disadvantage rules.
- **Targeting and duration:** the effects apply to **each creature** in the cone, including allies caught in it; success prevents that effect. Enervating ends at the **start of the Dragonborn's next turn** (not the target's next turn). Repulsion's push is instantaneous, while Prone persists until ended under its own condition rules. Do not add collision damage or obstacle bypass automatically.
- **Chromatic comparison:** Chromatic Warding uses a full Action once per Long Rest and replaces existing 50% elemental resistance with 100% for one minute, but only for the selected damage type; it is exceptionally strong when that damage is frequent and offers no increased protection against unrelated damage. Metallic control applies immediately to a group, but is saving-throw-dependent, shorter-lived, and requires a favorable cone position. Neither is categorically stronger in every encounter. Their different action costs and likelihood of relevant enemies need playtesting.

## Selected individual inherited adaptations — research rationale

| Dragonborn | Inherited damage | Selected adaptation | Main source and deliberate adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Brass** | Fire | **Sand Burrower:** 15-foot Burrow Speed through loose natural sand only, not rock, compact soil, built structures, or water; no permanent traversable tunnel. Ordinary suffocation, visibility and terrain restrictions still apply unless another rule explicitly changes them. | Native Brass Dragon Wyrmling has a 15-foot Burrow Speed. Sand restriction deliberately narrows a potent exploration/combat capability. Historical alternative: the 3.5e Dragon Shaman's self-only *Endure Elements* overlaps the current fire-resistance-based extreme-heat saving-throw exception and is not recommended without a separate temperature-system review. |
| **Bronze** | Lightning | **Amphibious:** breathe air and water indefinitely, no automatic Swim Speed. | 3.5e Dragon Shaman water breathing and native Bronze Dragon Wyrmling Amphibious. Mechanically comparable to native Black and Green. |
| **Copper** | Acid | **Natural Climber:** 15-foot Climb Speed, no ceiling movement or Spider Climb; extraordinarily difficult surfaces may still require checks. | 3.5e Dragon Shaman self-only *Spider Climb* is substantially stronger; native Copper Dragon Wyrmling has a Climb Speed. Starting with the already selected Red Dragonborn speed is an intentionally conservative adaptation. |
| **Gold** | Fire | **Amphibious:** breathe air and water indefinitely, no automatic Swim Speed. | 3.5e Dragon Shaman water breathing and native Gold Dragon Wyrmling Amphibious. Distinction from Bronze is elemental/family breath, not a required unique adaptation. |
| **Silver** | Cold | **Soft Landing (self-only):** as a Reaction when falling, slow your own descent and avoid falling damage if you land while the effect persists. At will, no passenger or ally protection and no ability to hover or fly. | 3.5e Dragon Shaman's self-only at-will *Feather Fall*, adapted to a discrete falling trigger rather than importing the spell wholesale. |

These adaptations are **selected native rules**, subject to future playtest-driven revision. Brass's sand/loose-terrain limits, Silver's reaction timing/duration, and any platform-specific Climb Speed behavior must be checked against existing native environmental and spatial mechanics during further mechanics integration. The staged `working/hazards/extreme-heat.json` already makes Fire-resistant creatures automatically succeed at heat-exposure saves, so adding a general Brass heat-protection trait without further design would largely duplicate its shared ancestry resistance. The alternative `working/hazards/extreme-cold.json` likewise grants an automatic save for Cold resistance, making temperature changes a broader environmental-rules question, not a simple one-leaf bonus. Burrowing can be much stronger than its speed number suggests; whether the sand restriction is tight enough remains an explicit open balance issue. Duplicate amphibious adaptations are not automatically a design defect; true-dragon ancestry and equivalent choices matter more than uniqueness for its own sake.

## Source distinctions and alternative breaths

- *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), Chapter 1: one Metallic family control breath with two selectable effects, **not** one effect per color. The control breath uses its own once-per-Long-Rest resource. Official publisher overview: https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1084-how-the-metallic-dragonborn-in-fizbans-treasury-of
- 3.5e *Player's Handbook II*, Dragon Shaman, Draconic Adaptation: Brass self-only *Endure Elements*; Bronze/Gold water breathing; Copper self-only *Spider Climb*; Silver self-only *Feather Fall*. These were class features, not automatically biological Species features. Cross-reference: https://srd.dndtools.org/srd/classes/basePhb2/dragonShaman.html
- 3.5e SRD true dragons and 5.5e native wyrmlings have **different color-specific secondary breaths** (e.g., Brass Sleep, Bronze Repulsion, Copper Slow, Gold Weaken, Silver Paralysis). These may inspire later selectable customization, but **do not stack** five additional color powers on the proposed shared family control breath. 3.5e SRD reference: https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/dragonTrue ; native source: `native/bestiary/{brass,bronze,copper,gold,silver}-dragon-wyrmling.json`.
- *Fizban's* Chapter 5 hoard themes have already been researched for all five Metallic types in `HOARD_TRADITIONS_RESEARCH.md`. They suggest **learned Upbringings**, not innate powers, mandatory hoarding, or alignment. No default pointer is selected yet.
