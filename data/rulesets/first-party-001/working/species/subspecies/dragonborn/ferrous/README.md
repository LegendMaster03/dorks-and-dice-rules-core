# Ferrous dragon research (deferred)

- Historical Ferrous family includes Iron, Cobalt, Chromium, Nickel, Tungsten.
- Several types overlap other edition taxonomies, especially Metallic. Do not treat all Ferrous dragons as a distinct immutable rules-family.

No additional playable Subspecies or Upbringing is approved here. Research potential classification, publication/source, inherited features versus transformations, and potential learned cultural traits before future reconciliation.
