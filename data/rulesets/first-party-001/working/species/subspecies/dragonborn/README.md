# Dragonborn future dragon types — research only

The **approved initial scope** is fifteen standard Dragonborn ancestries (five Chromatic, five Metallic, five Gem). Recursive Species/Subspecies inheritance and nested family organization are approved, but shared Breath Weapon and family mechanics are not yet reconciled. Do not publish playable definitions from these directories.

The notes below are **classification research**, not native Species, Subspecies, powers or lore decisions. Assignments vary by source/edition. Source material is in `data/reference/` where licensed, with additional books requiring separate source consultation.

**Deferred:** Platinum/Ruby/exceptional composite ancestry or rite-of-rebirth transformations; Dragon Shaman candidate feat to borrow curated traits from other ancestries; Dragonfire Adept-derived feats or prestige classes.
