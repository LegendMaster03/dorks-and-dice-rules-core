# Dragonborn research — active first pass and deferred families

## Active reconciliation
**Ten ordinary playable Subspecies:** Chromatic (Black, Blue, Green, Red, White) and Metallic (Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold, Silver).

Approved structural inheritance: Dragonborn Species → nonselectable Chromatic/Metallic family Subspecies → one playable dragon-type leaf. Stable parent IDs determine inheritance; folder nesting only organizes files. Shared Dragonborn traits are in `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`. The Chromatic family, geometry, Warding and five playable leaves are now approved under `native/species/subspecies/dragonborn/`. Metallic family traits and its five leaves remain unresolved.

See `chromatic/FIZBANS_REVIEW.md` and `metallic/FIZBANS_REVIEW.md` for published 5e mechanical options and potential enhancements. The reconciled base Species was promoted out of `working/`. The pinned SRD versions remain in `data/reference/srd/`; the unresolved generic historical Subspecies candidate remains at `working/species/subspecies/dragonborn/unnamed-1a909417769c0dff.json` **only because it contains Metallic source variants still requiring reconciliation**. See `SHARED_BREATH_REFERENCE.md` for the unselected alternatives.

The [*Fizban's* hoard source index](HOARD_SOURCE_INDEX.md) now links **20/20** researched Chapter 5 dragon and draconic-creature entries: five Chromatic, five Metallic, five Gem, plus five other draconic creatures recorded under [`unclassified/`](unclassified/README.md). Hoard themes are **research for learned traditions**, not Species biology or mandatory personality traits.

## Deferred
Gem family (Amethyst, Crystal, Emerald, Sapphire, Topaz) retains a reserved position in the *structural* hierarchy only. Exceptional Platinum/Ruby/composite Dragonborn, transformation rites, other dragon categories, Dragon Shaman feat ideas and Dragonfire Adept-derived advancement are outside this first pass.

Research notes are not playable definitions. Individual dragon affinity does not automatically prescribe culture, loyalty, alignment, or Upbringing. Approved coherent mechanics will move to native as they are selected.
