# Dragonborn research — active first pass and deferred families

## Active reconciliation
**Ten ordinary playable Subspecies:** Chromatic (Black, Blue, Green, Red, White) and Metallic (Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold, Silver).

Approved structural inheritance: Dragonborn Species → nonselectable Chromatic/Metallic/Gem family Subspecies → an individually selectable dragon-type leaf where one has been reconciled. The nonselectable Gem family now has its own native record, but no playable Gem leaves. Stable parent IDs determine inheritance; folder nesting only organizes files. Shared Dragonborn traits are in `native/species/base/dragonborn.json`. The Chromatic and Metallic families, their respective breath mechanics and all ten playable leaves are approved under `native/species/subspecies/dragonborn/`. Comparative playtest balance and Upbringing remain open.

See `chromatic/FIZBANS_REVIEW.md` and `metallic/FIZBANS_REVIEW.md` for published 5e mechanical options and potential enhancements. The reconciled base Species was promoted out of `working/`. The pinned SRD versions remain in `data/reference/srd/`; the original generic SRD Subspecies import remains at `working/species/subspecies/dragonborn/unnamed-1a909417769c0dff.json` only as a historical source/manifest record pending provenance-manifest cleanup; **it is not an alternate rule or a sign that Metallic mechanics remain unresolved**. See `SHARED_BREATH_REFERENCE.md` for the unselected alternatives.

The [*Fizban's* hoard source index](HOARD_SOURCE_INDEX.md) now links **20/20** researched Chapter 5 dragon and draconic-creature entries: five Chromatic, five Metallic, five Gem, plus five other draconic creatures recorded under [`unclassified/`](unclassified/README.md). Hoard themes are **research for learned traditions**, not Species biology or mandatory personality traits.

## Deferred
Gem family is now a nonselectable native intermediate Subspecies with no selected family mechanics or playable leaves. Amethyst, Crystal, Emerald, Sapphire and Topaz remain deferred. Exceptional Platinum/Ruby/composite Dragonborn, transformation rites, other dragon categories, Dragon Shaman feat ideas and Dragonfire Adept-derived advancement are outside this first pass.

Research notes are not playable definitions. Individual dragon affinity does not automatically prescribe culture, loyalty, alignment, or Upbringing. Approved coherent mechanics will move to native as they are selected.
