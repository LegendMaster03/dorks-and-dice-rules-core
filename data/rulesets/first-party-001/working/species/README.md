# Species taxonomy — working content

Only unresolved Species/Subspecies research and source candidates remain in `working/`. Reconciled, selected records belong in `native/species/`.

Base Species records that have been approved are now authoritative native JSON; do not duplicate their operative mechanics here. The pinned, original SRD material is retained in `data/reference/srd/` and its coverage manifest tracks each staged or promoted entity's current destination.

Use independent stable `id` and explicit `parent` relationships. Subspecies may inherit from another Subspecies; nested directories are organization, not the inheritance contract. Species and Upbringing inherit independently.
