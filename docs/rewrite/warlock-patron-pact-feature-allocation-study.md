# Warlock: Patron–Pact–Invocation feature allocation across ten Patrons

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Status:** **DESIGN INVESTIGATION — NO NEW MECHANICS OR ENTITLEMENT TRANSFERS APPROVED.** Research considers ten published 5.x Patron *concepts*, but the **only playable Patron in the current SRD-only vertical slice remains Fiend**. Binder/Vestige expansion and the status of a separate Hexblade class are *not* being implemented. Continue completing **Pact of the Blade, Chain, and Tome** while using this study to avoid structural mistakes.

**Question:** Can the Patron pillar be made less overloaded, while the Pact pillar becomes a consequential and independently advancing specialization? Hexblade–Blade overlap is an obvious example; investigate whether comparable refactoring works for other Patrons *without making Blade/Chain/Tome-specific Patron features mandatory, giving duplicate abilities, or invalidating existing published character concepts.*

## Executive finding

**Yes, but not by extracting equivalent amounts of power from every Patron.** Most published Patron features express the *supernatural source* rather than the *way the Warlock uses it*. Moving all features with a combat, spell, or companion expression from Patron to the similarly named Pact would break published non-Blade, non-Chain, and non-Tome builds. The useful precedent in Hexblade is specifically **generic martial competency** (proficiency, Charisma weapon attacks) that properly belongs in Blade. Hexblade's source-specific **curse, specter, and defensive powers** are not automatically Blade powers.

The more robust design is **separation of feature origin from manifestation channel**:

- **Patron core / source:** Signature source identity, spells, traits, unique curses, transformation, healing, elemental affinity, and any signature resource. Available to eligible followers of that Patron *regardless of Pact*, unless that feature's own approved restrictions say otherwise.
- **Pact chassis / delivery:** A substantial, generally Patron-neutral specialization in **Blade (weapon), Chain (Pact familiar), or Tome (grimoire/ritual casting)**. It has level progression that does not require buying ordinary Invocations to function.
- **Patron × Pact expression:** When truly appropriate, an existing Patron-granted effect could be *manifested* through the chosen Pact (e.g. blade, familiar, grimoire) with the **same underlying feature entitlement/resource**. This is **NOT** a new grant, not mandatory across Patrons, and not an approved change to the Fiend's already-settled mechanics.
- **Pact-attached Invocation:** An optional clause with typed Pact and/or the Patron **of that same Pact** as prerequisites; it consumes an approved Invocation choice. It can improve a Pact or introduce a specialized technique; the Patron decides what terms they accept. It is not used to backfill required baseline Pact progression.
- **Actual transfer / relocation:** Reserve for a *genuinely generic* feature duplicated between published Patron and the now-independent Pact; delete or supersede the old native grant **only after** validating a single entitlement. Preserve old source provenance/preset behavior; do not give either side a compensating feature without owner approval.

This allocates **power origin, progression grant, attachment/manifestation, and presentation** independently. A visual theme (flame, shadow, tentacle, spectral form) is not itself evidence that the ability belongs to Blade, Chain, or Tome.

## Pact catalog extension: Talisman and Star Chain (owner-approved 2026-10-25)

This original Patron allocation study was authored for **ten Patron concepts × Blade/Chain/Tome** (30 conceptual pairings). The owner subsequently expanded Pact reconciliation to include **Talisman** (official non-SRD 2020) and **Star Chain** (2016 WotC Seeker-specific UA playtest), producing a conceptual **ten × five = fifty** combinations for *eligibility screening*. **The original 30-pair research does not itself validate the extra twenty combinations**. Consult [five-Pact scope](warlock-five-pact-reconciliation-scope.md).

**Talisman overlay (10 reference pairs):** There is **no named-Patron requirement in the published Talisman Pact Boon**. A Fiend, Archfey, Celestial, Great Old One, Fathomless, Genie, Hexblade, Undead, Undying, or Vestige relationship can therefore be examined for a Talisman Pact *subject to its own terms*. Talisman is a protective transferable amulet and must not duplicate existing Patron defensive/healing resources or automatically add its three optional Invocations. These ten pairs are **eligible-in-principle design cases, not individually playtested or automatically permitted by a particular Patron**.

**Star Chain overlay (10 reference pairs):** The **original UA specifically requires a character dedicated to the Seeker**. None of the ten Patron **categories alone** establishes that source-defined link. **Do not declare the 10 ordinary Patron+Star Chain pairings legal merely because a Patron is willing to offer it**, or infer that Archfey/Great Old One/Celestial/Genie knowledge themes waive this requirement. A character could conceivably have a Seeker-linked specific Patron *within* a broad category, but a mapping from traits/category to Seeker eligibility must be **explicitly approved later**. The **Seeker itself is not an in-scope playable Patron** and was not one of the ten reference Patron concepts. Preserve synthetic Seeker-linked fixtures for testing the eligibility contract, with no actual playable Seeker import.

**Fiend-only runnable slice:** Blade, Chain and Tome are the existing three Fiend+Pact paths; **Talisman adds a fourth Fiend+Pact design case**, pending its own native mechanics. **Star Chain adds a negative test case for Fiend** until a Seeker-linked eligibility exception or playable Seeker Patron is separately approved. Do not call these five legal Fiend build paths. Star Chain's level-5 development is unsolved, not inherited from BG3.

## Classification criteria

For **each individual source feature**, answer in order:

1. **Source identity test:** Would this power still make sense for a **Tome** or **Chain** Warlock of that Patron? If yes, moving it **exclusively** to Blade is probably wrong; analogous questions apply to Chain/Tome.
2. **Generic-method test:** Could a Warlock with a *different* Patron reasonably perform it because they chose the **same Pact**? If yes, consider Pact-chassis status. This is a *candidate*, not a blanket rule.
3. **Dependency/uniqueness test:** Is it a distinct named Patron resource, a tiered Patron feature, or a prerequisite for later Patron features? If so, preserve its origin, timing, and resource ledger even if it has a Pact-shaped manifestation.
4. **Role test:** Is it a fundamental recurring capability needed for Blade/Chain/Tome competence (**Pact**), a signature source power (**Patron**), or an elective enhancement (**Invocation**)?
5. **Eligibility test:** Does the option reference **the Patron attached to the actual Pact** and the same Patron's accepted terms, not any other Patron on the character?
6. **Budget/source fidelity test:** Can both 2014 and 2024 (and later 2026) original source builds be reconstructed with *one copy* of every approved native effect, the same meaningful resource cost, no hidden free bonus, and no forced Pact switch?
7. **Severance/transfer test:** If the Pact is broken, does the attached Invocation become unavailable while the acquisition history remains? A transferred Invocation must still meet the receiving Pact/Patron conditions. Rules for Patron feature loss and multiple-Pact acquisition remain out of scope.
8. **GM/UX test:** Can guided and advanced builds explain available and unavailable combinations without forcing active, personally signed negotiations? Existing/inherited terms are valid.

### Results by likely transfer category

1. **High-confidence duplicate/overlap (one example):** Hexblade's **Hex Warrior weapon-use chassis → already native Blade**. Do not grant it a second time through a Hexblade preset.
2. **Conditional, feature-specific expression (most cases):** Most Patron abilities retain ownership but could later have Pact delivery variants **without duplicate resources**. Strong contexts are Archfey mobility, Celestial support, Great Old One psychic contact, Genie elements, Undead manifestations and selected Fiend techniques. These are **design ideas, not implicit free upgrades**.
3. **Similar-looking but separate effects:** Fathomless tentacle, Great Old One aberration summon, Hexblade's Accursed Specter, Vestige Companion, ordinary Chain familiar, and Tome *Animate Dead* grant are **different underlying capabilities**. Shared infrastructure is appropriate; merging their mechanical entitlements is not.
4. **Poor transfer candidates:** Genie Limited Wish, Fiend Hurl Through Hell, Celestial resurrection, Undying longevity and the Fey/GOO signature source identities should not automatically be moved merely to equalize pillar counts.
5. **New invocation candidates:** Source-restricted *enhancements* to Blade, Chain, Tome (e.g., shadow weapon curse, fiendish familiar rider, celestial tome support) may be good future design; **none** is granted or written as playable content by this study.

## Deep example: Hexblade is a partial precedent, not a universal conversion rule

Original 3.5e **Hexblade** in *Complete Warrior* was a **separate base class**. In 2014, the published **Hexblade Patron** drew from a Shadowfell supernatural source associated with sentient weapons; it did *not* mandate the 2014 Blade Pact. *Xanathar's Guide to Everything* split generic Hex Warrior abilities and distinct Patron curses/specter into one subclass.

**Mechanically safe decomposition hypothesis:**

- Existing native **Pact of the Blade** already owns magical weapon binding, proficiency and CHA-based attacking, with intrinsic level-5 second attack. **Never re-grant Hex Warrior equivalents for selecting Hexblade.**
- Keep **Shadowfell power source** in the Patron dimension. A specific Patron could be mysterious/unrevealed; the blade is not universally the Patron.
- Keep **Hexblade's Curse** as a source-related power pending comparison with **Great Old One's 2024 Eldritch Hex** and other curse features. If made elective, keep its special effect ID, prerequisites and resource ledger; **do not let a plain Blade Pact automatically acquire a Hexblade curse**.
- **Accursed Specter** is *not* automatically Chain: a defeated enemy raised as a specter is different from the familiar the player selects. The Warlock may legitimately be Blade or Tome and still gain a specter via their supernatural source. Companion behavior should use shared entities, command, targeting and resource systems.
- The result might be a guided **Hexblade-flavored Warlock build preset** rather than a new universal Pact or a direct copy of the older subclass. **Preset identity/costs stay unapproved.**

**Counter-test:** Design both a 2014-style **Hexblade + Tome** and **Hexblade + Chain**, with source-recognizable curses and no Blade Pact. If the proposed decomposition can only reconstruct Hexblade + Blade, it fails source fidelity. A different future story may allow a Shadowfell Patron to grant any Pact while reserving certain curse-related clauses as patron-restricted Invocations; this is a possibility, not a new ruling.

## Four 2024 Patron cases: strongest near-term architecture validation

1. **Archfey:** Patron owns the Steps of the Fey progression and Misty Step effects regardless of Pact. Pact-specific delivery styles can refer to **one shared teleport-use resource**; no separate teleport charges for weapon/familiar/book.
2. **Celestial:** Patron owns the Healing Light **dice pool**, Radiant Soul and Searing Vengeance. Any Pact-specific support/radiance option must refer to that same pool or pay its own Invocation budget.
3. **Great Old One:** Patron owns mental-communication/psychic damage and related curse features. **Create Thrall's Summon Aberration** is a separate summoned creature, not a Chain familiar; test two concurrently controlled units and both abilities' turn/resource controls.
4. **Fiend (only executable-in-scope Patron):** Keep **all previously approved milestones** and resource decisions. Dark One's Blessing, Dark One's Own Luck, **one Infernal Retaliation feature with exactly two mutually exclusive effects sharing one Long Rest use**, and Hurl Through Hell **do not relocate** without further owner approval. The 13-spell Fiend union, optional learned-only mode, and retired Fiend level-10 damage resistance **remain unchanged**. No invented patron→pact grant or refunded selection. The Fiend is sufficient to test **one Patron × three Pacts**, but insufficient to claim balance across all ten Patrons.

## Recommended implementation architecture — no unauthorized grants

An effect need not be moved into a new pillar merely because it is *used through* that pillar. Use a generic **capability identity** with separately indexed **grant**, **source**, **manifestation**, **Pact affiliation**, **resource** and **dependencies**.

Suggested **semantic fields / relationships** (not mandated JSON property names):

- `effectId`: stable native mechanic; **one executable definition** for an effect.
- `grantEntitlement`: records **which pillar and progression milestone** actually grants it; changing expression does not award another entitlement.
- `powerSourcePatron`: source identity or eligible Patron type; an actual Patron individual may remain unrevealed.
- `pactAgreementId`: the **specific** Pact owning an attached Invocation or carrying a manifestation.
- `manifestationChannel`: `direct`, `weapon`, `familiar`, or `grimoire` (illustrative only), so narrative expression need not change ownership.
- `patronAcceptanceTerms`: accepted Pact/Invocation clauses distinct from **game-mechanical prerequisites**.
- `resourceLedgerId`: the shared rest, action, charges, spell-slot or dice resource that every channel must consume.
- `prerequisiteTypes`: Patron, same Pact type, features, level, and accepted contract terms, all evaluated on initial acquisition and restoration/transfer.
- `history` / `availability`: track suspended Invocations and provenance through Pact loss; existing Patron features are not automatically suspended unless a separate approved rule says so.
- `sourceProjection`: 2014/2024/2026 or historical 3.5 feature descriptions preserved as references without reproducing restricted text.

**Important distinction:** The agreement binds **Invocations** to a Pact per approved owner rule; do **not** infer that every **Patron** feature expires when an individual Pact terminates. The exact effect of a broken Pact on source-granted Patron power is a separate open design decision. Do not silently impose an across-the-board Patron suspension rule.

## Reconciliation and acceptance plan

### Case A: In-scope Fiend × all three Pacts (mandatory)

- Create three *hypothetical* builds: **Fiend+Blade, Fiend+Chain, Fiend+Tome** using the *same authoritative approved Fiend* effect definitions.
- Validate Warlock levels **1, 5, 6, 8, 10, 13, 14, 18 and 20** (Fiend feature milestones plus Pact scale) without duplicate grants. Fiend feature level 10 remains intentionally absent; universal Warlock **Energy Resistance** remains at 10.
- Confirm initial Warlock 1 Pact is dedicated, not an Invocation expenditure; three Pact upgrades at 5 apply **one per character's chosen Pact**, not all three.
- **Fiend + Blade**: CHA weapon benefit does not duplicate Hex Warrior and second attack replaces source Thirsting Blade purchase; Hurl Through Hell attacks use source eligibility, not a Blade-only rewrite.
- **Fiend + Chain**: Pact familiar and its attack actions follow owner's companion-on-player-initiative rule. Fiend Blessing's 10-ft cooperative trigger is not automatically extended to distant familiars.
- **Fiend + Tome**: Tome's three free level-3 spell uses at 5 do not consume or duplicate the Fiend 13 automatic spell grants; retain spell durations and concentration requirements.
- All Invocation options validate against *attached Pact and the Patron bound to that Pact*, and Patron willingness/terms. When the Pact ends, attached Invocations become unavailable without deleting acquisition history.

### Case B: Reference-only 10 × 3 compatibility matrix (no new playable content)

For **all ten published Patron concepts**, model Blade, Chain and Tome (30 conceptual pairings). Unless *a particular option* has a documented Patron/Pact restriction or a specific Patron refuses in the setting, **do not assert the pairing is illegal**.

For each pair, record: (a) a recognizable source-PATRON signature, (b) the Pact's independent base and level-5 advancement, (c) whether signature summons/curses are separate from Pact familiar/weapon/book, (d) any shared resource and prerequisite interactions, (e) 2014/2024/2026 provenance, (f) whether a guided source-style build is reconstructible without hidden Invocation taxes.

Stress combinations **Hexblade+Tome, Hexblade+Chain, Genie+Blade, Fathomless+Tome, Vestige+Chain, Great Old One+Chain, Celestial+Blade, Archfey+Chain, Undead+Tome, Undying+Blade** specifically. Use non-playable fixtures; do not author a non-SRD active Patron.

### Case C: Feature relocation scoring before asking the owner

For any proposed move, record a compact decision card with:
1. **Exact source feature** and era
2. **Current native grant owner**, effect/resource ID and granted level
3. **Proposed new location**: Pact base, Patron remains, optional Invocation, or Patron×Pact expression
4. **Source build counterexamples** broken by the move
5. **Duplicate/underpowered/overpowered effect and cost comparison**
6. **Ability after original Pact breaks / Invocation transferred** and applicable Patron acceptance
7. **Recommended disposition** and **explicit owner approval required**

A transfer is **not approved** simply because a feature *could* be narratively delivered by a familiar, weapon or grimoire.

## Assessment

**Finding:** Three distinct Pacts can support widely varied Patrons **without requiring a wholesale migration of the Patrons' signature powers**. Hex Warrior is an unusually good candidate for true migration because it supplies **generic Blade competence** already supplied by the 2024 baseline. For most other Patrons, preserve source powers and selectively offer **Pact manifestations** or Patron-restricted **Invocations** if they add meaningful choice without duplicating resources.

**Immediate next step stays unchanged:** finish the base mechanics and intrinsic advancement of Blade, Chain and Tome; integrate them first against the **approved Fiend** and validate the three Fiend+Pact paths. Revisit candidate cross-Patron expressions and Hexblade classification **only after their own source-preserving decision cards and owner authorization**. Binder/Vestige gameplay-system migration is specifically out of scope.
