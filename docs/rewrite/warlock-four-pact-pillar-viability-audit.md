# Four-Pact pillar viability audit: Blade, Chain, Tome, Talisman

**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** **COMPARATIVE DESIGN ASSESSMENT ONLY**. This audit **does not approve new Pact abilities, remove Invocations, alter spell/resource budgets, or activate native mechanics**. Earlier owner approvals are preserved. Star Chain and its Seeker Patron remain **disabled/deferred**, with historical research retained.

**Question:** After reconciling D&D 5e 2014, 5e 2024 and the owner-approved *Baldur's Gate 3* Deepened Pact improvements, have the four active Pacts become sufficiently **substantial, differentiated and independently advancing** to be a third major Warlock pillar alongside Patron and Pact-bound Invocations?

## Owner clarification: a Pact's Invocation ecosystem is part of its substantive value

**Owner correction (2026-10-25):** A Pact is **not judged primarily by how many free class-level features it gains**. Its depth includes **what Invocations may be attached to it**, how those clauses combine and specialize the base agreement, and which Patron relationships accept or enable the clauses. This clarification **supersedes any statement in the earlier audit implying every Pact must gain mandatory intrinsic features at Warlock 11, 17 or 20**.

**Correct assessment model:**

1. **Base Pact and existing automatic advancements:** Unique permanent role and sufficiently usable basic capability. Preserve approved Blade/Chain/Tome automatic 5th-level upgrades; no new intrinsic level milestones are implied.
2. **Pact-exclusive and Pact-dependent Invocation branches:** Number, depth, level spread and *meaningful distinct builds* created by optional attached clauses. Invocation acquisition is a genuine investment, not a sign the Pact is incomplete unless a particular Invocation is required simply to operate the baseline.
3. **Shared eligible Invocation pool:** Even Invocations **without a Pact-type prerequisite attach to the selected Pact** by owner's approved architecture, so a Talisman or Chain user can build a unique suite of general powers around that Pact. These Invocations do **not** become free Pact grants, and their presence is **not by itself evidence of four different Pact-exclusive catalogs**.
4. **Patron requirements and willingness:** A Pact can gate Invocations by **the Patron of that very Pact**, and the Patron can accept/refuse certain clauses. A Fiend+Blade, Fiend+Talisman or a different future Patron combination can yield different legal attachment pathways. Restrictions remain per option; do **not** invent universal Fiend/Blade-only gating.
5. **Sensible baseline / no mandatory tax:** An Invocation may be extremely strong and remain elective. Only if *every functional build* requires it for basic attacks, companion commands, rituals or talisman utility should relocation into the core Pact be examined.
6. **Advancement by chosen investments:** A Warlock can gain a meaningful higher-level Pact specialization by attaching later Invocations without receiving a separate mandatory Pact feature at the same level. Assess 1–20 progression from **Pact + attached Invocation choices**, while still evaluating intrinsic baseline separately.

## Executive assessment

**The three original SRD Pacts are credible major specializations**: they have differentiated foundations, intrinsic 5th-level upgrades and several **Pact-specific Invocation paths**. Their level-1-to-20 viability must be evaluated as **base Pact + attached optional Invocations + Patron eligibility**, not just automatically granted features. No higher-tier intrinsic Pact grants are approved; **that is not necessarily a deficiency**. **Talisman** now has an **owner-approved native level-1 synthesis and level-5 Shared Ward**, plus three published elective Invocation branches. Its runtime implementation and choice-path balance tests remain outstanding. The issue is not whether all defining specializations cost Invocation choices, but whether **at least one useful core function exists without a mandatory tax**.

## Structural qualification standard

A fully substantial Pact should meet all five criteria:

1. **Recognizable identity** independent of selecting a Patron; its signature behavior changes player decisions in play.
2. **Self-sufficient baseline competence:** a Warlock is not functionally forced to buy an optional Invocation to perform the Pact's core job.
3. **Meaningful progression through the Pact's attached Invocation tree and/or intrinsic Pact advancements** throughout the Warlock's career; automatic features at every tier are **not required**.
4. **Meaningful agency beyond the selection:** weapon choices, familiar forms/roles, book knowledge and rituals, or talisman assignment/defense are real choices with costs/consequences.
5. **Cross-Patron portability:** generic Pact competence does not appropriate source-specific Fiend or other Patron powers. Restrictions/Patron willingness remain per option.

**A Pact is not required to have as many automatic features as a Patron**, or be numerically equivalent at every level. Evaluate its recognizable baseline plus **electively attached Invocations** at 1, 5, 11, 17 and 20; at higher levels an Invocation can legitimately supply substantial progression. The issue is *forced purchases for baseline operation*, not optional specialization purchases. These are **evaluation checkpoints**, not approved new feature levels.

## A. Pact of the Blade

### Historical sources
- **2014 SRD 5.1:** dedicated level-3 Pact Boon: action to conjure an interchangeable melee weapon; magic for nonmagical resistance/immunity; magic weapon 1-hour bond ritual and extradimensional storage.
- **2024 SRD 5.2.1:** level-1 Invocation: Bonus Action conjure/bond, proficient Pact weapon used as spellcasting focus, Charisma attacks and damage, choose normal/Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant damage. No equivalent extradimensional weapon-storage ritual.
- **BG3:** automatic level-5 Deepened Pact second Pact-weapon attack, optional bonding of an **ordinary mundane** weapon; anti-disarm is **research only**, not owner-approved.

### Native owner decisions
- **Approved** 2024 baseline, ordinary mundane melee weapon binding as magical, and **intrinsic second Pact-weapon attack at Warlock level 5** without Invocation expenditure.
- **Unresolved** 2014 ritual and extradimensional retrieval, sentient/artifact restrictions versus 2024 attunement, complete mixing of magical resistance traits, ability to bond ranged magic weapons, anti-disarm/throw restrictions, source Thirsting Blade duplicate disposition and Devouring Blade prerequisite.
- Source **Lifedrinker**, **Devouring Blade**, and **Eldritch Smite** remain optional Invocation candidates and **are not intrinsic Pact features**.

### Is Blade substantive enough?
**At levels 1–10: largely yes**, because level 1 enables the martial route and level 5 does not require the Thirsting Blade selection tax. **At level 11–20: unproven**. Eldritch Blast's beam count increases at 11 and 17, while the approved Blade extra attack remains **two total**. At equal Invocation investments, compare expected chance-to-hit, magic weapons, feats, reaction/bonus actions, range, enemy AC, and damage-type resistance. **Do not automatically give a third/fourth attack or stack 3.5e iterative attacks** to correct a presumed gap.

**Candidate development directions, not rulings:** revive the distinct 2014 long-term bond/storage, weapon recall and identity; add Pact-defined higher-level weapon versatility, defensive/positioning or tactical techniques; reserve expensive smites, particular curse types and exceptional damage riders for Invocations or Patron-specific expressions. **Melee viability** is the baseline test, **raw peak damage parity with Blast** is not automatically the sole goal.

## B. Pact of the Chain

### Historical sources
- **2014 SRD 5.1:** level-3 Pact Boon: `Find Familiar` as ritual without using a learned spell, four special forms (imp, pseudodragon, quasit, sprite); forgo one of your Attack action attacks to let familiar make one **Reaction** attack.
- **2024 SRD 5.2.1:** free-slot **Magic action** casting of `Find Familiar` with expanded forms; retains **Attack action sacrifice → familiar Reaction attack**.
- **2014 SRD Invocation:** `Voice of the Chain Master` extends familiar senses/communication within plane; `Chains of Carceri` is a level-15 special hold effect on certain creature types.
- **2024 SRD Invocation:** `Investment of the Chain Master` at level 5 grants bonus-action command to Attack, movement form, necrotic/radiant attack damage option, Warlock spell save DC for familiar's saves and defensive reaction resistance. These are **not owner-approved automatic base Pact benefits**.
- **BG3:** level-5 Deepened Pact familiar **extra attack** (owner-approved native direction).

### Core issue
The source Pact's **familiar Reaction attack** triggered by sacrificing the Warlock's own attack is not the same as **the familiar taking the Attack action** in its own right. Our native level-5 approval is **a second familiar attack when the familiar can attack**, but it cannot be executable without resolving *how and when the familiar is directed to take that action*. Beware of a false second attack that grants nothing because the familiar has no independent Attack action under a strict reading, and the opposite problem of doubling familiar attacks **on both player reaction-trigger and independent familiar turns** inadvertently.

**Relevant owner policy:** Controlled creatures normally act on the **player's initiative**. We must define whose action/Bonus Action/Reaction supplies the command, whether the familiar acts on its own turn, and which ability permits its attacks when no Invocation is selected. Do not infer a new extra player attack, two full attack actions, or a free unlimited second summoned unit.

### Is Chain substantive enough?
**Role and narrative:** very strong. The familiar supports scouting, familiar-specific magic, positioning, touch-spell delivery, combat support and independent characterization. **Mechanically without Investment:** uncertain. Special familiars vary greatly in attack success, durability, resistances, save DCs and ability to meaningfully participate at level 11+. The **level-5 extra attack alone does not ensure survivability or high-level progression**.

**Candidate improvements, not rulings:** explicit universal Pact familiar command/control baseline; sensible scaling for familiar hit points, attack/ability save scaling and defense keyed to Warlock levels; alternate familiar support roles for non-damage play; later tier upgrades in mobility, senses, or coordination. Keep investment-specific movement/damage defenses as elective unless a mandatory baseline gap is proven.

## C. Pact of the Tome

### Historical sources
- **2014 SRD 5.1:** level-3 Book of Shadows with **three cantrips from any class**, including a 1-hour replacement ceremony; `Book of Ancient Secrets` Invocation supplies **two level-1 rituals and future found-ritual transcription scaling up to half Warlock level (rounded up)**.
- **2024 SRD 5.2.1:** at-rest conjured Book of Shadows with **three cantrips and two level-1 rituals** selected when the book appears, from any class, and spellcasting-focus use. The 2024 basic Tome does **not** explicitly grant the 2014 Book of Ancient Secrets' permanent higher-level ritual transcription and collection system.
- **2024 SRD Invocation:** `Gift of the Protectors` at Warlock 9 is an optional names-in-book protection against one 0-HP event per Long Rest. **Not an intrinsic Pact grant.**
- **BG3:** level-5 Deepened Pact grants **one free slotless cast each** of `Animate Dead`, `Haste`, and `Call Lightning` per Long Rest; owner approved this as intrinsic Pact progression.

### Core issues
1. The **original 2014 Book of Ancient Secrets scalable ritual learning** is not the same thing as the **2024 two level-1 rituals available while the book exists**. The latter provides immediate utility, but its ritual level **does not scale** in the imported Pact entry. As a knowledge/ritual specialization, Tome needs evaluation of whether sustained ritual acquisition is core competence or an optional Invocation.
2. The owner has already eliminated **daily prepared spells** system-wide. The 2024 Tome's selected book spells may still be a **book-granted availability list**, but **must not be translated into mandatory preparation**. Book creation/rewriting timing and held-book dependence need a coherent native mechanic.
3. The three free level-3 spell uses at Warlock 5 are **independent resource entitlements**, distinct from Fiend Patron spells, ordinary Warlock spells and Pact Magic slots. `Haste` and `Call Lightning` have concentration implications; `Animate Dead` interacts with our unified creature and initiative control. The free-spell package may be stronger in encounter burst and out-of-combat breadth than Blade or Chain's recurring attacks; compare across realistic adventuring days/rest counts rather than simply counting features.

### Is Tome substantive enough?
**Yes in thematic distinction and levels 1–5**, assuming 2024 core is reconciled. **Beyond level 5**, its granted level-3 magic does not scale by itself; at Warlock 11–20, merely retaining three 3rd-level free castings may become comparatively less exciting. The *knowledge* function also plateaus absent a ritual system. It should not need a compulsory Invocation purely to behave like a ritual/grimoire specialization.

**Candidate directions, not rulings:** give Tome a tiered *knowledge/ritual acquisition* baseline drawing carefully from 2014 Book of Ancient Secrets; keep optional rare/specialized tomes, protective pages and extra spellcasting techniques as Invocations; test concentration and Warlock spell access before adding further free high-level spells.

## Talisman strengthening direction — subsequently owner-approved (2026-10-25)

The owner has directed us to **bring Talisman up to the other three Pacts before proceeding to the next Warlock pillar**. A [dedicated Talisman strengthening proposal](warlock-talisman-pact-strengthening-proposal.md) compares the 2019 UA's unproficient-skill +1d4 without a stated daily cap against the 2020 published after-failure +1d4 (PB uses/Long Rest). It **now records an owner-approved** nonstacking level-1 synthesis plus a level-5 **Shared Ward** that reduces damage with a Reaction while consuming the same Pact charge pool; see the [approved decision](warlock-talisman-pact-strengthening-proposal.md). The existing optional **Rebuke, Protection, and Bond of the Talisman** Invocations retain distinct retaliation, saving throw, and teleport specializations; none becomes free.

**The owner approved this design.** It replaces the earlier assumption that Talisman's three Invocation branches must automatically be rolled into its baseline. The adopted target is **a sufficiently useful base + an intrinsic level-5 feature + elective Invocation paths**. Existing historical source inventories and owner-approved levels remain unchanged.

## D. Pact of the Talisman

### Historical sources
- **2019 WotC UA Class Feature Variants:** initial Talisman prototype granted a **1d4 on ability checks for skills the wearer lacked proficiency in**, without the later *Tasha's* failed-check/proficiency-use framework. The playtest is historical evidence, not the published native baseline.
- **2020 published *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*:** level-3 Pact Boon: a transferable amulet; **after the wearer fails an ability check**, they can add **1d4** to possibly succeed, **proficiency-bonus uses per Long Rest**; an hour ceremony replaces the amulet. Unlike 2019 UA, it is not conditioned on lack of skill proficiency.
- **Published optional Invocations:**
  - `Rebuke of the Talisman`: psychic retaliation and pushing an attacker.
  - `Protection of the Talisman` (Warlock 7+): bonus on a **failed saving throw**, with its own defined use pool.
  - `Bond of the Talisman` (Warlock 12+): teleport between Warlock and talisman's wearer, conditional and limited.
- **2024 SRD 5.2.1:** **no Talisman entry** as a Pact or Invocation; 2024 baseline has only Blade, Chain and Tome.
- **BG3:** no Talisman/Deepened Pact counterpart.

### Is Talisman substantive enough?
**Yes, provisionally, after the owner's native design approval.** Talisman now has unlimited nonproficient-skill check support, scarce after-failure ability-check rescue, and an intrinsic level-5 protective Reaction that consumes the same charges. Its published **Rebuke, Protection, and Bond** Invocations still provide distinct optional retaliation, failed-save defense and teleportation. This is now a credible support Pact; actual runtime behavior and equal-budget balance remain untested.

**Important role distinction:** Talisman need not compete with Blade in DPR. It should be evaluated as a **protective/support/mobile-bond specialization**, including usefulness between fights, ally agency, action/Reaction limits, and wearer-change consequences.

**Owner-approved resolution:** Deepened Pact: **Shared Ward** is a distinct Dorks & Dice level-5 mitigation feature, not published by WotC, and it **does not duplicate the published optional Protection of the Talisman** saving-throw ability. Later design directions (retaliation, mobility, higher-level ward scaling) remain **unapproved**; optional high-impact Invocations are not freely granted.

## Patron relationship and packaging

- Fiend's approved **13 Patron spells, Dark One's Blessing, Dark One's Own Luck, Infernal Retaliation (two effects / one use), Hurl Through Hell**, and the native Warlock's universal Energy Resistance remain unchanged. Do not move them into Blade/Chain/Tome/Talisman under this audit.
- Pact-specific effects and optional Pact-bound Invocations can have requirements on that Pact's **Patron relationship** or accepted terms. Apply them to the **specific Pact** in a potential future multi-Pact build, not just the global identity.
- Warlock level 1 gives **one dedicated first Pact**, no Invocation cost. Additional simultaneous Pacts remain an *advanced concept without decided gates*. Do not presume all four or convert them into Invocations.
- Historical **Star Chain and Seeker** remain disabled; do not incorporate their mechanics into Tome/Talisman through this review.

## Criteria for completion of the four-Pact pillar (proposed acceptance matrix)

**Structural acceptance** (can be tested now from design decisions):
- Each Pact's acquisition and identity is modeled separately from Patron and Invocations.
- Each uses one class-level-1 Pact entitlement; no Invocation selection spent.
- All four are available as active **reconciliation candidates**, not claims of executable content.
- Invocations are attached to one Pact and may add a requirement on the same Pact's Patron; Patron consent/terms and mechanical prerequisites both checked.
- Star Chain/Seeker options never appear as active options.

**Mechanical acceptance** (not yet achieved):
1. Decide Chain and Tome base feature definitions from source (2014/2024). **Talisman's native base is owner-approved**; implement and validate it.
2. Resolve **Chain familiar command/attack action** and its level-5 two-attack trigger; match the owner's player-initiative rule.
3. Resolve **Tome book/ritual growth**, free 3rd-level spell uses, concentration, animated dead, and no-prepared-spells rule.
4. **Implement and test approved Talisman Shared Ward** at Warlock level 5, retaining Rebuke/Protection/Bond as distinct optional Invocations without duplicate grants.
5. Define intentional progression into later level bands (evaluate Warlock 11 and 17) for **all four** or explicitly justify why a specific Pact remains effective without new inherent powers.
6. Equal-resource, equal-level cross-Pact tests at Warlock 1, 5, 11, 17, 20 under **Fiend only**; compare **combat contribution, resource flexibility, exploration, support, protection, command load, rest-cadence dependence, and role uniqueness**, not just expected DPR.
7. Source-preserving projection tests: 2014 and 2024 Blade/Chain/Tome source mechanics, BG3 approved Pact progressions, *Tasha's* Talisman sources and associated optional Invocations. Historical license categories unchanged.
8. Build/editor/UI tests: choose one Pact at level 1, hide ineligible Pact/Invocation options, describe Patron agreement restrictions, and show familiar/book/talisman trackers only when needed.

## Decision sequence suggested for owner

1. **Confirm evaluation standard**: Pact must stand alone at level 1–5 and remain meaningfully relevant through 20, while Invocations add choice rather than core viability. This is a recommendation, *not yet a new global approval*.
2. **Resolve Chain** command/attack/turn semantics **before** adding more familiar damage.
3. **Resolve Tome** whether a scalable ritual repository belongs to its basic Pact rather than requiring the 2014 Book of Ancient Secrets Invocation.
4. **Implement the approved Talisman** level-1 check package and level-5 Shared Ward, and validate its retained optional Rebuke/Protection/Bond Invocation paths.
5. **Revisit Blade** high-level comparison to Eldritch Blast and whether weapon bond/restoration or other tactical utility supplies the missing Pact identity.
6. **Only then** consider additional high-level Pact upgrades or Patron × Pact expressions; do not balance by moving Fiend Patron features without explicit consent.
