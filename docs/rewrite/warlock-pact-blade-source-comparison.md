# Warlock Pillar 2 — Pact of the Blade: 3.5e, SRD 5.1, SRD 5.2.1 comparison

**Status:** Source comparison complete; **mechanical reconciliation NOT owner-approved**. No native executable Pact of the Blade rule is activated by this document. Prepared October 25, 2026 for the current SRD-only Warlock vertical slice.

**Previously approved Pact architecture remains controlling:** Every native Warlock chooses a **separate, dedicated Pact at Warlock level 1**, consuming no Invocation choice. An Invocation is an additional clause attached to one Pact, and may require the Pact's type and/or its associated Patron. Patron acceptance under applicable terms and explicit mechanical eligibility are separately validated. Future additional Pacts remain a deferred advanced option; their level/cost/maximum is not resolved. See [Pact pillar reconciliation](warlock-pact-pillar-reconciliation.md).

## Source version coverage

- **D&D 3.5e** (*Complete Arcane*, 2004): **no Pact of the Blade**, nor Blade/Chain/Tome Pact Boons. The Warlock used blast-shape Invocations; relevant historical mechanics include **Hideous Blow** (*Complete Arcane*) and **Eldritch Glaive** (*Dragon Magic*). These are historically relevant **Invocation candidates**, not historical Pact grants.
- **D&D 5e 2014, SRD 5.1:** **Pact of the Blade** is a separate Warlock Pact Boon acquired at **level 3**.
- **D&D 5e 2024, SRD 5.2.1 (5.5e):** **Pact of the Blade** is a first-level-available Eldritch Invocation, consuming an Invocation selection in its source edition. Native Dorks & Dice **does not copy that acquisition cost**.

## Direct Pact mechanics

| Topic | 2014 SRD 5.1 | 2024 SRD 5.2.1 | Native reconciliation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial acquisition | Level 3 separate Pact Boon | No level prerequisite, acquired as Invocation from level 1 | **Already approved:** dedicated Pact at Warlock level 1; no Invocation spent |
| Conjuring action | **Action** in empty hand | **Bonus Action** in hand | Open: activation action; favor evaluating 2024 usability |
| Conjured weapon forms | Chosen **melee** weapon; form chosen on each creation | **Simple or Martial melee** weapon | Open: permitted forms; preserve melee-conjuration distinction |
| Weapon proficiency | Proficient while wielded | Proficient while bond lasts | Closely aligned |
| Attack/damage ability | Normal weapon rule, generally Strength/Dexterity; base Pact **does not grant Charisma substitution** | May use **Charisma** instead of Strength/Dexterity | Open: whether 2024 benefit is native base Pact or elective enhancement |
| Alter damage type | Weapon's normal type; treated magical for overcoming nonmagical resistance/immunity | May inflict normal type or **Necrotic, Psychic, Radiant** | Open: type conversion and explicit handling of magical attacks under mixed-edition defenses |
| Spellcasting focus | Not part of base Pact | Bonded weapon is a Warlock **Spellcasting Focus** | Open: adopt 2024 focus benefit? |
| Bond magic weapon | **1-hour ritual** holding a magic weapon | **Bonus Action**, touching a magic weapon | Open: speed, permanence, and restrictions |
| Magic ranged weapon | Official Sage Advice clarifies a **bonded magic** weapon need not be melee, although conjured weapon must be melee | Official 2024 Sage Advice preserves same distinction; **bonded magic** weapon need not be melee | Open: consistent source-preserving rule; do not accidentally prohibit magic ranged weapons |
| Magic weapon exclusions | Cannot use bonding ritual on **artifact or sentient weapon** | Cannot bond weapon attuned to **someone else** or **bonded to another Warlock** | Open: whether to use both safeguards, and interaction with native attunement |
| Storing bonded magic weapon | Explicit **extradimensional storage and recall** from 1-hour ritual | **No equivalent storage/recall language** in base Invocation | Open: restore 2014 convenience, make optional upgrade, or retire |
| Ending bond | 1 minute more than 5 ft away; dismiss, replace, die; bonding another and unbond rituals for magic weapon | 1 minute more than 5 ft away; reuse Bonus Action; die; conjured weapon disappears at end | Open: exact unbond/replacement and historical item ownership behavior |

**Important 2014/2024 distinction:** Neither source permits freely **conjuring a nonmagical ranged weapon**. An already-existing **magic ranged weapon** may instead be bonded. Official 2024 Sage Advice explicitly confirms the distinction.

**Note on existing magic weapon ownership:** The 2024 Pact has a short bond action but does not explicitly reproduce 2014's 1-hour permanent weapon ritual, extradimensional storage, or item retrieval. Do not infer those effects automatically from the source.

## Pact-linked Invocations — separate from the first Pact

| Option | 2014 SRD 5.1 | 2024 SRD 5.2.1 | Reconciliation note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Thirsting Blade** | Warlock **5+**, Pact Blade; Attack action with Pact weapon grants **two attacks total** | Warlock **5+**, Pact Blade; **two attacks total** | Same core function; retain as an optional Invocation candidate rather than automatic Pact upgrade unless separately approved |
| **Lifedrinker** | Warlock **12+**, Pact Blade; **Charisma modifier (minimum 1) Necrotic** damage on **every Pact-weapon hit** | Warlock **9+**, Pact Blade; **once per turn** add **1d6 Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant** and may expend a Hit Point Die for healing | Mechanically different: throughput, healing, resource, damage choices and level require owner decision |
| **Devouring Blade** | **Not in SRD 5.1** | Warlock **12+**, requires Thirsting Blade; increases to **three attacks total** | Native advanced chain candidate; do not automatically grant or stack with 3.x iterative attacks |
| **Eldritch Smite** | **Not in SRD 5.1** (later supplements outside this 2014 SRD slice exist) | Warlock **5+**, Pact Blade; **once per turn** spend Pact Magic slot for Force bonus damage and possible Prone (Huge or smaller) | In-scope 2024 optional Invocation candidate; check short-rest slots and native 0-HP/Prone rules |

These Invocations remain **Invocation-pillar options**, with **Pact of the Blade as a prerequisite/affiliation**; they do **not** become free Pact features. Author-specific Patron restrictions can be added per Invocation when justified, but no universal Fiend-only restriction is approved.

## 3.5e historical melee Blast relationships (research only)

- **Hideous Blow** (*Complete Arcane*): Least blast-shape Invocation channels Eldritch Blast through a **single ordinary melee weapon attack**, with both the weapon and Eldritch Blast damage on a hit. The official 3.5e FAQ specifies that attack and blast occur in the **same standard action** and that use of the spell-like Invocation can provoke an attack of opportunity. It is **not a Pact weapon-conjuration feature**.
- **Eldritch Glaive** (*Dragon Magic*): Least blast-shape Invocation manifests Eldritch Blast as a **melee reach/touch attack**, using a **full-round action**; may benefit from historical iterative attacks and threatened-square attacks of opportunity. It **does not add weapon damage** to Eldritch Blast. It is **not** a bound physical weapon.
- **Reconciliation direction to evaluate, not an approval:** Keep both as potential advanced **Eldritch Blast shape Invocations**. Never auto-grant to all Pact of the Blade Warlocks or stack 3.5e iterative, 2024 Devouring Blade, full weapon damage, and full Eldritch Blast damage without an explicit balance rule.

## Decision cases for owner, in logical order

1. **Base Pact:** Use the 2024 Bonus Action, proficiency, Charisma attack/damage, spellcasting focus, and selectable Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant type as native default? If not, identify which elements are elective.
2. **Existing magic weapon bonding:** Preserve 2014's hour-long bonded-weapon ritual and extradimensional storage, use 2024 quick touch-bond, combine under distinct rules, or relegate the old ritual/storage to an optional Pact enhancement? Clarify 2014 artifact/sentient and 2024 attunement/bond exclusions.
3. **Weapon form:** Keep both sources' distinction between conjured melee and bonded existing magic weapons (including ranged); specify when damage conversion/Charisma applies to bonded ranged weapons.
4. **Magical weapon treatment:** The 2014 Pact explicitly overcomes mundane resistance/immunity, while 2024 offers damage-type conversion. Native Warlock and monsters preserve mechanics from multiple editions; resolve both explicitly.
5. **Pact Invocation progression:** Reconcile two-attack Thirsting Blade (5+), Lifedrinker (12+ vs 9+, once-per-hit vs once-per-turn), and 2024 Devouring Blade/Eldritch Smite, preserving normal Invocation budgets and signed Pact/Patron eligibility.
6. **3.5e heritage:** Defer Hideous Blow and Eldritch Glaive until the Invocations pillar, after core Pact weapon attacks and Eldritch Blast balance are established.

## In-scope acceptance tests (future implementation)

- All native Warlocks may choose Blade at **Warlock level 1** with **one dedicated Pact entitlement**, no spent Invocation.
- Pact must remain attached to an allowed Patron; Patron terms may forbid a particular Pact, and Invocation eligibility must be evaluated against the **same Pact's** Patron.
- Pact termination suspends attached Invocations; transfer requires a valid receiving Pact/Patron with accepted terms, never automatic carryover.
- Represent a conjured melee weapon separately from a bonded existing magic weapon; enforce chosen safeguards on item attunement, ownership, special properties and weapon form.
- Compare Blade weapon builds to **base Eldritch Blast** at Warlock **1/5/11/12/17/20**, with and without optional Invocations; avoid accidentally granting extra attacks more than once.
- Apply Dorks & Dice base action economy and approved critical and damage rules, not 3.x full-round/multiple attacks by default.
- Maintain a native pending-reconciliation status until approved effect and resource definitions are tested.
