# Wizard Band 1 reconciliation

**Status (2026-10-24): Complete declarative Wizard levels 1–20.** Source import is preserved; full runtime is deferred.

The native class uses the modern d6 Hit Die, Intelligence spellcasting and Intelligence/Wisdom saves, full-caster spell slots (or optional spell points), modern cantrips (3 at level 1, 4 at 4, 5 at 10), simple weapons, Arcane Recovery, Ritual Adept and Scholar Expertise.

**Spellbook:** Starts with six level-1 Wizard spells and acquires two more eligible spells each subsequent Wizard level (44 at level 20 before copied additions). New Wizard spells can be copied for 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level; backups use 1 hour and 10 gp per spell level. No daily preparation; previously learned eligible spells remain ordinarily castable after the physical book is lost. Ritual Adept and other explicitly book-dependent activities still require the physical book. Open Spell Access remains optional and does not grant more resources.

**Historical 3.x retained:** Level-1 Scribe Scroll as a free automatic class feature; Wizard Specialization selections at **5, 10, 15 and 20** from eligible shared Metamagic and Item Creation options. The Wizard may also replace any otherwise legal general-feat selection with one eligible specialization option. Option prerequisites and repeat restrictions apply; no Sorcery Points are granted. Crafting, feat mechanics and costs remain shared-system work.

**2014 Spell Mastery at level 18:** Select a level-1 and a level-2 Wizard spell in the spellbook. Each is castable at lowest spell level without a slot; upcasting pays normal cost. Replace one or both after eight hours of study. Daily preparation is removed. The historical 3.x Spell Mastery feat is excluded from the specialization list.

**Signature Spells at level 20:** Two level-3 spells from the book, each castable once at level 3 without a slot, recharging on Short or Long Rest; higher-level casts pay normal resources.

**2024 Memorize Spell at level 5:** Inactive because prepared-spell swapping is redundant.

**ASI levels:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 19. Epic Boons require total character level 21+. **Subclass milestones:** 3, 6, 10, 14; no subclass features activated. Familiar path is mutually exclusive but its mechanics remain deferred.

**Accounting:** 16 active native feature records (11 reconciled imported plus 5 3.x native additions), 10 preserved inactive imported historical/subclass records, 1 reconciled base class. Converted 12 imported 5.x records (9 multi-candidate, 3 single-candidate). The pinned 5.x coverage manifest still tracks exactly 2,522 source records; 3.x native additions are not falsely labeled 5.x imported content.

**Sources:** `data/reference/srd/structured/class/class-wizard.json`, [3.5e Wizard](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/sorcererWizard.htm), `docs/rewrite/native-rules-design-specification.md`.

Detailed Metamagic costs, crafting, subclass and familiar mechanics, higher bands, and executable runtime consumers remain deferred without blocking this baseline.
