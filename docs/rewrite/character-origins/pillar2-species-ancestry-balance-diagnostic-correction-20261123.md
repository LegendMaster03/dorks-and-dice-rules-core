# Pillar 2 — Species and Ancestry Balance Diagnostic Correction

**Date:** November 23, 2026

**Scope:** Correct the independent read-only report `pillar2-species-ancestry-balance-diagnostic-20261123.md` after recovering November 22 owner decisions and implementing ordinary Dragonborn genes. This addendum supersedes the diagnostic's contrary approval assessment and nominal Dragonborn totals. Its recorded historical scenario measurements are not being represented as rerun.

## Approval correction

The earlier diagnostic incorrectly concluded that ordinary Dragonborn Natural Weapons and Wings were only research estimates. The owner approved both for ordinary Dragonborn on **November 22, 2026**. They are distinct from exceptional Platinum and Fivefold wings and Gem Spectral Flight.

| Gene | Cost | Implemented ordinary mechanic |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `trait.draconic-natural-weapons` | 1 | Claws 1d6 Slashing; bite 1d6 Piercing; tail 1d6 Bludgeoning. Strength attack and damage, attack proficiency, 5-foot reach; corresponding anatomy required. Ordinary melee attacks only, no extra attacks, Bonus Action attack, or special Shove |
| `trait.draconic-wings` | 5 | Glide from level 1 up to 30 feet horizontally per turn with at least 5 feet of descent per 20 feet horizontal; safe landing prevents falling damage and deployment needs space. From level 5, fly at 30 feet for up to 1 minute once per Long Rest without hovering. Glide remains; continuous flight never arrives |

These are canonical Species genes under `native/origination/biological/genes/` and are inherited through the Dragonborn parent chain, not copied across leaf Ancestries. The versioned policy is `biological-pricing-20261123-v2`, still with an **18-point** provisional creation allowance. This is canonical **trait and creation-ledger implementation**, not a claim of completed live combat or flight runtime execution.

## Implications and remaining boundaries

- The lower ordinary Dragonborn totals were primarily a **missing approved-content problem**, not evidence of inherent weakness.
- The diagnostic's claim of zero approved missing ordinary Dragonborn content is superseded. Its measured historical fixture results were derived from a package *without* the new natural weapons or Wings; these outcomes should not be used as complete-package gameplay comparisons.
- The large residual spread, including Rock Gnome at 8 and Drow at 18, remains **unvalidated as a difference in gameplay power**. No other Species characteristic is added solely to fill the budget.
- The five known unsupported Halfling/Hill Dwarf trait shapes, the High Elf/Human choice evidence, Wood Elf source override, and universal player-facing host integration are tracked separately.
- Task 3 is a **provisional balance diagnostic**. Follow-up playtesting is needed to evaluate relative utility across classes, levels, environmental scenarios and mixed origins.

This correction does not reopen unrelated pricing decisions, alter the inherited/expressed gene limits, authorize exceptional Ancestries for ordinary creation, or implement the separate Character Sheet/Rules Wiki integration.
