# Pillar 3 — Acolyte Background Reconciliation

**Status:** Source-verified initial comparison and **unapproved** proposed native mechanics. No Background promotion, content edits or executable work authorized by this document.

**Base:** `main` at `d5a2b15b7ae2bd88ae9e47a5ddfad01d481623bb` (merged Pillar 1 PR #130). **Branch:** `feature/character-origins-pillar3-life-experiences`.

## Purpose

Use Acolyte to test the owner's proposed design method: 2014 5e is the practical starting Background framework; optional 3.5e Character Background provides additional ideas for role, contacts, and specialization; 2024/5.5e grants are reviewed individually for Upbringing, Background, or wider Character Origins allocation.

The resulting Background need not match either edition numerically. Future 5.x import/conversion maps a character into Dorks & Dice rather than reconstructing 5.x mechanics exactly.

The 2014 feature includes conditional religious care/hospitality, modest lifestyle support for the acolyte, and possible local temple aid. The source explicitly distinguishes presiding over rites from channeling divine power. Full SRD references:
- https://5e.d20srd.org/srd/backgrounds.htm
- https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/personality-and-background
- https://dungeontoolbox.com/library/dnd-2024/backgrounds/

**2014 customization evidence:** The general 2014 Background rule permits substituting a feature, choosing two skill proficiencies, and choosing a total of two language/tool proficiencies from sample Backgrounds. It also replaces a duplicate skill/tool proficiency with a different same-kind proficiency. These are historic comparison rules; no identical Dorks & Dice substitution semantics have yet been approved.

**3.5e variant evidence:** The optional Character Background variant is an activity-by-level character-generation procedure, not an ordinary level-1 Background package. Its Religious Background entries include multiple roles (temple leader, evangelist, sacred librarian, keeper of the dead, cultist, inquisitor, exorcist, temple guardian, cloistered) with relevant training, contacts, gear, rank/recognition, and possible feat recommendations. It is useful evidence for diverse religious roles, but its explicit class-level, feat-level, rank, reputation and spending rules should **not** be imported automatically. Source: https://www.d20srd.org/srd/variant/buildingCharacters/characterBackground.htm

## First candidate: 2014-derived Acolyte core

**Not approved; proposed starting package for interactive reconciliation:**

- **Identity:** Previous involvement/service within a religion, spiritual tradition, or analogous organized religious community. Not automatically a cleric, magical caster, full institutional member in current good standing, or a character raised within that faith from infancy.
- **Skills:** Insight and Religion training, through existing universal competency/qualification semantics.
- **Languages:** Initially retain two learned-language choices as the *2014 comparison baseline*. Do not automatically relocate them into Upbringing or silently remove them because Upbringing may already grant a learned language. Whether the final package has two choices, a more flexible learned-training choice, or another allowance remains open pending cross-package tests.
- **Feature:** Retain Shelter of the Faithful as a candidate **social/relationship feature**, not a feat or unconditional magical effect. Access to hospitality, support, ceremonies, and help depends on relevant persons/institutions, location, and relationship standing; a Background acquired in the past does not force every institution to assist indefinitely. Quantities/limits and how support is granted in play remain to be reconciled.
- **Equipment:** Use a selected starting package or purchase/resource alternative, with all items and currency added to ordinary inventory. 2014 and 2024 packages must not both be awarded.

## Individually evaluate 2024 additions

### Calligrapher's Supplies

Relevant to a religious scribe or calligrapher, but not inherently necessary for a temple guardian, cantor, itinerant preacher, or unlettered novice. Candidate: an optional Acolyte specialization or an ordinary learnable competency, potentially a bounded replacement within an acquired-training allowance. **Not** an automatic addition until benefit accounting is agreed.

### Magic Initiate (Cleric)

A useful example of religious magical education, but Acolyte does not imply spellcasting or a divine spell list. A 2014 temple functionary, a purely social acolyte, and a cultist need not receive the same magical package. Candidate: recommended Origin feat/specialization for an appropriately trained character **if** the eventual general feat grant system offers such a selection. Do not automatically grant extra feats because a character chooses both Acolyte and a detailed Upbringing. Preserve existing independent feat grants from other native rules.

### Ability scores

A 2024 Acolyte receives a constrained +3 total, unlike the 2014 Background. Do not append this to a 2014 Background package as a free gain. Keep the eventual initial ability-score allocation as a general Character Origins question pending separate design (and separate from explicitly inherent traits), rather than locking every Acolyte into the three 2024 attributes.

### Equipment and social privilege

The 2024 equipment alternatives are replacement starting packages, not additive resources. The removal of Shelter of the Faithful from 2024 does not by itself show that institutional relationships have no place in Dorks & Dice. Conversely, adding the 2014 social feature to a feat-granting 2024 package must be evaluated against the general benefits of other Backgrounds.

## Upbringing versus Background: applied tests

**Lifetime religious upbringing and service:** The same years can inform both Upbringing and Background. Neither component needs an artificial age boundary. However, a repeated learned competency/language from both packages should not silently grant two proficiency multipliers or ranks. A cross-package substitution policy is still pending.

**Cross-cultural devotee:** An Orc raised in an Elven community may learn Elvish through its chosen Elf Upbringing, then learn other languages or Religion through the Acolyte Background. Its Orc physiology/creature type are unchanged.

**Acolyte who leaves their order:** Learned skills/languages remain acquired history; current organizational privileges may change with relationships and access. Do not define all Shelter rights as irrevocable passive powers.

**Religious scribe versus temple guardian:** Both may have Acolyte Background; one might learn Calligraphy, the other martial or protective competencies through another ordinary rule. Neither is required to learn Magic Initiate (Cleric) merely to qualify as an Acolyte.

**Upbringing with existing Religion or Insight training:** If such an Upbringing becomes available, duplication needs explicit native semantics. The 2014 fallback is useful evidence but not automatically binding.

## Design decisions for user review (not assumed approved)

1. **First proposal:** Preserve a 2014-style conditional social/institutional feature for Acolyte, with relationship standing and actual access distinguished from permanent learned capabilities. Decide whether this remains a defined Background benefit or becomes optional relationship content.
2. Decide whether two learned-language choices remain Acolyte's default, change into a limited choice among culturally/religiously acquired languages or competencies, or move into a common Life Experiences allowance. Do not force a particular answer from the existence of language-granting Upbringings.
3. Decide how Origin feats are allocated globally before attaching Magic Initiate to Acolyte, and how specialized calligraphy/other training can vary.
4. Agree on starting equipment policy and cross-source duplicate learned grants.
5. Only then draft a selected native Acolyte JSON record, maintaining source conversion relationships and explicitly marking non-executable behavior.

## Interpretation boundaries

This document is **analysis**, not permission to change `native/`. Avoid creating a general social-network/reputation subsystem just to support Acolyte. Do not interpret a religious affiliation as a Species trait, supernatural inheritance, guaranteed spellcasting or necessarily current institutional standing. Do not infer approval of a universal numeric budget, origin feat allocation or fixed Upbringing structure.
