# Pillar 2 — Fivefold Chromatic Dragonborn: exceptional ancestry design

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** **Source-led proposal for owner review; no mechanics approved.** The exceptional composite identity and acquisition boundaries are already approved. Do not promote this document to selected-native gameplay or ordinary mixed-Heritage eligibility.

## Approved identity and acquisition boundaries

Fivefold Chromatic is **one exceptional Dragonborn ancestry** formed through the direct personal participation of the Chromatic category sovereign in Draconic Rebirth (or express DM authorization). Its constituents are the **five fixed founding Chromatic houses: Black, Blue, Green, Red and White**. When further ordinary Chromatic houses are authored, they **do not automatically become constituents** of this exceptional form.

The Rite **replaces** the entire previous Species/Ancestry-derived intrinsic package, retaining historical contributor/parentage evidence, Background, Upbringing and independent class benefits. This does **not** select five ordinary ancestry leaves, stack five complete trait packages or allow exceptional powers in standard mixed-Heritage candidate pools. It is intentionally substantially stronger than a normal starting ancestry.

**Proposed content parentage:** Represent Fivefold as a separate singular exceptional leaf directly under Dragonborn Species (or equivalent explicit override path), thematically Chromatic but **not automatically inheriting an ordinary leaf's single-affinity binding or its ordinary Chromatic Warding without replacing the relevant rules**. The exact native layout belongs to future implementation.

## Primary candidate: Fivefold Elemental Sovereignty (not approved)

**Breath:** **One** exceptional Breath Weapon and **one PB/Long Rest resource** replacing the ordinary Dragonborn's damage breath. Each use chooses **one** of Acid, Lightning, Poison, Fire or Cold, rather than granting five different breath attacks. Starting proposal: one consistent **30-foot cone** with the native Dexterity save (DC 8 + PB + Constitution modifier, half on success) and a total-character-level damage progression to review alongside Platinum's already approved **2d10 / 4d10 / 6d10 / 8d10** at levels **1 / 5 / 11 / 17**. These numbers and the cone are *reference candidates*, not authorized copying of Platinum's progression. Historical native Chromatic **30-by-5-foot line** is another viable choice; do not infer that an Area parameter may arbitrarily choose both line and cone without a new approved rule.

**Resistances:** Direct reference for simultaneous **50% resistance to all five defining damage types** (Acid/Lightning/Poison/Fire/Cold), replacing the ordinary single leaf-linked 50% resistance; **not five stacked or cumulatively applied 50% instances**. This is considerably stronger than any ordinary Chromatic Dragonborn, intentionally so. Need assess whether the legendary ancestry receives all five immediately or on a staggered level progression.

**Warding:** One **Chromatic Warding** activation, **not** five independent uses. Primary candidate: at the Warding threshold, 1 Action and 1 use per Long Rest grant **100% resistance to all five corresponding damage types for one minute**, replacing each normally active 50% resistance rather than adding percentages. A secondary candidate allows one chosen type per activation, but is much less distinctive if the character already resists all five. Retaining native **level-5** threshold is straightforward but not yet selected for the exceptional form.

**Dread/fear:** The champion's necrotic + fear breath provides a potential *additional expression*. Three approaches for future owner comparison:
1. Keep the single five-affinity breath, **no fear rider**; greater range of elemental damage and warding define the ancestry.
2. Add a **limited frighten rider** on that one breath (with a defined save and timing), inspired by the champion but without converting the damage to necrotic.
3. Make the champion-style **necrotic fear breath** the core instead, and represent five-element sovereignty through resistance/warding; this sacrifices the source's obvious five-affinity breath identity.

**Shared exceptional champion defenses:** The 5e Dragonborn champions of Tiamat, Bahamut and Sardior each have fear immunity and one use/day Legendary Resistance. Whether Fivefold also gets a bounded failed-save intervention and immunity to native **fear-sourced** conditions is a separate decision. Platinum's already approved Mind defenses do **not** automatically extend to Fivefold or Ruby.

**No approved extra anatomy, flight, spellcasting, regeneration, legendary actions, five simultaneous breaths, all-element damage on one use, or extra weapon attacks.** These would each require separate approval. The fact that the sovereign can manifest them as a monster is not sufficient to make them inherited character traits.

## Decision order for a complete mechanical pass

1. Choose the **breath identity**: five-selectable types vs necrotic/fear alternative; one use and one resulting damage type are the recommended baseline.
2. Choose the **defense model**: five simultaneous resistances, one-type defensive mode, or a staged progression.
3. Choose **Warding semantics** and how the ordinary inherited Chromatic family mechanics are replaced rather than duplicated.
4. Decide whether **fear rider, fear immunity, limited failed-save protection or other physical characteristics** are intrinsic to this reborn ancestry.
5. Reconcile **numeric level progression** by **total character level**, including a character reborn after level 5, 11 or 17, and document resource replacement.
6. Validate the design against Platinum's approved package without imposing ordinary character-creation balance parity.

## Explicit non-goals

Do not reproduce the copyrighted leader as a playable fictional character, associated cult story, mythic narrative, five literal heads or monster stat block. Player-facing first-party material should refer descriptively to the **Chromatic sovereign** and its **Fivefold** descendants. The source names in this research note are provenance only. Do not implement this proposal in native JSON before owner acceptance.
