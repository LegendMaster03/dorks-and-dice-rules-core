# Pillar 2 — Fivefold Chromatic Dragonborn: exceptional ancestry design

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** **Owner-approved Fivefold core and staged Chromatic Flare/Wrath progression (2026-11-11)**: one Breath Weapon choosing among five founding Chromatic damage types and two whole areas, simultaneous 50% resistance to all five, one enhanced Warding, Wings at levels **1–4 / 5–10 / 11+**, **automatic Chromatic Flare from level 1** when first reduced to 0 HP, and **level-11 Chromatic Wrath** adding HP recovery and an immediate-or-delayed Flare choice. **Breath Weapon damage dice, Warding unlock level, and proposed fear/save defenses remain undecided.** No exceptional native gameplay or ordinary mixed-Heritage eligibility has been activated.

## Approved identity and acquisition boundaries

Fivefold Chromatic is **one exceptional Dragonborn ancestry** formed through the direct personal participation of the Chromatic category sovereign in Draconic Rebirth (or express DM authorization). Its constituents are the **five fixed founding Chromatic houses: Black, Blue, Green, Red and White**. When further ordinary Chromatic houses are authored, they **do not automatically become constituents** of this exceptional form.

The Rite **replaces** the entire previous Species/Ancestry-derived intrinsic package, retaining historical contributor/parentage evidence, Background, Upbringing and independent class benefits. This does **not** select five ordinary ancestry leaves, stack five complete trait packages or allow exceptional powers in standard mixed-Heritage candidate pools. It is intentionally substantially stronger than a normal starting ancestry.

**Proposed content parentage:** Represent Fivefold as a separate singular exceptional leaf directly under Dragonborn Species (or equivalent explicit override path), thematically Chromatic but **not automatically inheriting an ordinary leaf's single-affinity binding or its ordinary Chromatic Warding without replacing the relevant rules**. The exact native layout belongs to future implementation.

## Approved Fivefold Elemental Sovereignty — core mechanics

**Approval recorded (2026-11-11):** Fivefold's composite nature is expressed through **choice of one founding Chromatic damage type on each Breath Weapon use**, rather than five separate breaths, plus five simultaneous defenses and one corresponding Warding feature. The approval covers the **underlying feature package**, not all missing numeric details.

### One breath, with a damage-type choice

- The exceptional Dragonborn receives **one Breath Weapon**, with **one normal PB-uses-per-Long-Rest resource**. This replaces the ordinary single-affinity Dragonborn Breath Weapon; it does **not** add five separate attack options with independent charges.
- **On each use**, choose exactly **one** damage type: **Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison**. A breath use deals **only that chosen damage type**, not five simultaneous damage types or an automatic mix of them. Different uses may select different types without a rest-based reconfiguration.
- Continue using the existing Dragonborn principle of **replacing one attack during the Attack action** and a Dexterity saving throw against **8 + PB + Constitution modifier** (half damage on success), unless the subsequent geometry/numeric pass establishes a justified exception.
- **On each use**, independently of the damage-type choice, select **one whole Breath Weapon area**: a **60-foot line, 5 feet wide**, or a **30-foot cone**. The line retains Chromatic's defining shape while extending its reach for an exceptional ancestry; the cone gives wider coverage. Both have the same attack-replacement, save, damage dice and shared resource. **Neither activation produces both areas.** The two shapes are expressly authored exceptional options, **not** an alteration of ordinary Chromatic's 30-by-5-foot line or permission to mix component dimensions in the ordinary mixed-Heritage rules.
- Damage scales by **total character level**, including at the level when rebirth occurs. **Exact dice remain undecided**; Platinum's 2d10/4d10/6d10/8d10 at 1/5/11/17 is a comparison, not an automatic grant.

### Five simultaneous resistances

- The exceptional ancestry carries **50% resistance to each** of Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning and Poison **at the same time**, rather than selecting one resistance or switching affinities between rests. This is an explicit **five-type defensive grant** replacing ordinary Dragonborn's single leaf-linked resistance, not five copies of the same general resistance rule.
- Damage resistance is applied to each qualifying damage component in the usual way. Resistances to a damage type **do not stack with themselves** and do not automatically confer immunity to associated conditions, environmental effects, or damage types outside the five founding houses.
- The Fivefold source list is **fixed** to the five founding houses; adding Brown, Purple, or other ordinary Chromatic dragon types later does **not** expand this ancestry's breath or resistances automatically.

### One enhanced Chromatic Warding

- **One Chromatic Warding activation** upgrades **all five approved 50% resistances to 100%** for the same **one-minute** duration. These percentages **replace**, rather than add to, the usual resistance for each of the five types.
- Preserve the ordinary native activation model of **one Action and one use per Long Rest**, with **one shared expenditure** for the entire five-affinity defense; there are not five independent Warding uses.
- **The level required to unlock this enhanced Warding remains undecided.** Native Chromatic Warding's level-5 threshold is the default starting point for the next numerical pass, not an independently approved level gate for the exceptional form.

### Other abilities still to evaluate separately

- The published *Dragonborn of Tiamat* champion's **necrotic/fear breath** is **not** the approved central Fivefold breath. Necrotic damage is outside the five established founding affinities. A **fear rider** on the existing single breath remains a possible *additional* feature, not automatically granted through the approval.
- **Fear-sourced condition immunity and a limited failed-save intervention** occur among published exceptional Dragonborn champions and in the approved Platinum design. Whether they apply to Fivefold is **not settled**; the shared source motif alone is not permission to copy them.
- Do **not** infer extra heads, extra bite attacks, spellcasting, monster Legendary Actions, or automatic imports of the sovereign's full stat block. **Wings and the bounded Chromatic Wrath/Flare sequence are now expressly approved exceptions.** Any later ancestry expression must be evaluated independently from a possible Dragon Warlock Patron.

**Interaction with the Rite:** Rebirth removes prior Species/Ancestry abilities and grants the approved Fivefold identity and its currently level-eligible benefits. It does **not** preserve an earlier ordinary Dragonborn leaf's resistance/warding as extra stacking resources. Historical parents, Background and Upbringing remain unchanged; no additional mixed-Heritage contributor route is created.

### Shared legendary Wings progression — approved timing

| Total character level | Approved Platinum and Fivefold Wings | Comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1–4** | **Glide** as already specified for Platinum: controlled descent, up to 30 ft horizontally per turn, descending at least 5 ft per 20 ft horizontally | Physical wings from rebirth |
| **5–10** | **30-ft flying speed for one minute, once per Short or Long Rest**, with glide still available | Ordinary Gem flight also unlocks at level 5, but is **once per Long Rest and permits hover** |
| **11+** | **Continuous 30-ft flying speed**, retaining gliding | Reach permanent flight one level earlier than the original Platinum threshold |

**Approved scope:** The revised shared progression **supersedes Platinum's earlier 1–5/6–11/12+ stages**. Both exceptional ancestries glide at **levels 1–4**, fly **30 feet for one minute once per Short or Long Rest at levels 5–10**, and fly **30 feet continuously at level 11+**. This is an express legendary Wings feature, not ordinary Chromatic inheritance. It does not grant hover, increased speed, movement while incapacitated or a second flight resource. Ruby remains separate.

**Rationale:** At level 5, legendary wings should at least reach the temporary powered-flight tier available to ordinary Gem Dragonborn; the once-per-Short-or-Long-Rest refresh is broader than the ordinary Gem once-per-Long-Rest use, while Gem retains its own hover identity. At level 11, sustained flight establishes a larger extraordinary advantage. A more radical level-1 flying speed is possible but would discard the previously approved stages and is unnecessary to fix the mismatch.

### Chromatic Flare and Wrath — authoritative approved progression

**Owner-approved revision (2026-11-11):** Chromatic Flare is the **foundational offensive last-stand ability**, available from character level 1. Chromatic Wrath is its **level-11 survival and controlled-release upgrade**. This **supersedes the earlier level-11-only Wrath-linked Flare design**; it does not add a second feature use or permit both the old and new versions.

| Total character level | Chromatic Flare damage | Trigger and resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1–4** | **1d10** | When incoming damage reduces the Dragonborn to **0 HP without killing them outright**, a Flare **immediately triggers automatically without an Action or Reaction**. The Dragonborn remains at 0 HP, falls Unconscious and makes death saving throws normally. **No HP recovery**. |
| **5–10** | **2d10** | Same immediate automatic Flare at 0 HP, with ordinary Unconscious and death-saving-throw handling; **no HP recovery**. |
| **11–16** | **3d10** | **Chromatic Wrath** activates on the same qualifying trigger and leaves the Dragonborn conscious at **HP equal to twice total character level**. Choose **immediate automatic Flare** or **hold Flare for up to one minute**, then use an Action to release it while able to act. |
| **17+** | **4d10** | Same Wrath recovery and immediate-versus-delayed choice. |

**Flare area, damage and targets:** One **15-foot-radius burst centered on the Dragonborn**. Choose which **other creatures within the area** are affected and choose **one** damage type from Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning and Poison **when Flare resolves**. Chosen targets make a **Dexterity saving throw against DC 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Constitution modifier**, taking **half damage on success**. The triggering Dragonborn is not a target. This is a separate, fixed burst; it does not use the exceptional Breath Weapon's line-or-cone area.

**Shared resource:** At all levels, this is **one use per Long Rest for the entire Flare/Wrath sequence**, not one low-level Flare plus an additional independent high-level Wrath. The trigger and any Flare released from it consume that single use. Flare does **not** consume a normal Breath Weapon charge, refill the Breath Weapon, restore Warding or other resources, grant bonus actions, or duplicate the burst. A later qualifying drop to 0 HP during the same Long Rest cannot trigger an already spent Flare/Wrath use.

**Levels 1–10 — 0-HP and death saves:** The native Unconscious condition imposes Incapacitated and prevents using Actions or Reactions. Therefore **Flare is an automatic effect of the qualifying HP-loss event**, resolving without an Action or Reaction; its normal target/damage-type selection is solely part of resolving that effect and does not grant general awareness or the ability to act while Unconscious. A creature that is **killed outright**, or whose drop to 0 HP is **prevented by some independent effect**, does not trigger this feature. After the burst, it remains at 0 HP and proceeds with **ordinary 5e-style death saves**, unless independent effects change that outcome. Damage taken while already at 0 HP does not provide another separate trigger.

**Level 11+ — Chromatic Wrath and flexible Flare release:** The same qualifying damage event now triggers Chromatic Wrath, preventing the 0-HP/Unconscious outcome and leaving the Dragonborn with **HP equal to twice total character level**. This is **one instantaneous HP restoration**, **not regeneration each round**, and does not cure conditions or replenish other abilities. The player chooses either (a) **Flare immediately, automatically, and without an Action**, or (b) **hold this one Flare for at most one minute**, releasing it with one legal Action while able to act. Flare **expires unused** if the minute passes, or the character can no longer release it before expiry. Holding Flare never postpones the HP recovery. The feature does not grant an extra off-turn Action or a reaction attack.

**Source and balance interpretation:** The progression adapts the *Aspect of Tiamat's* death-triggered mythic retaliation to character scale. From levels 1–10, this is an aggressive **death-throes burst, not protection from defeat**. From level 11, recovery and tactical timing add resilience and control without a second health pool. At all levels the feature's offensive value depends on **reaching the qualifying 0-HP event**, so it does not function as a proactive once-per-rest attack. Ordinary Dragonborn do not gain it.

**Rite and late rebirth:** A character gaining Fivefold at any level receives the corresponding **total-character-level** tier immediately; a level-14 rebirth has 3d10 Flare and the 28-HP Wrath response without starting at the level-1 version. Any already-spent resource transition across rebirth remains part of the shared Rite system rather than special free refresh logic for Fivefold.

**Implementation boundary:** This **complete staged progression is approved as a design**, superseding the old level-11-only timing; native record promotion and executable condition/HP transition semantics await Pillar 2 integration. In particular, the engine must resolve the low-level triggered burst consistently with standard 0-HP and Unconscious timing, without treating the automatic burst as a normal Action.

### Consolidated Fivefold identity — approved features and remaining proposals

- **Elemental sovereignty:** Already-approved five type choices and two areas for the one damaging breath; **2d10/4d10/6d10/8d10** at level 1/5/11/17 remains the leading unapproved numerical candidate.
- **Chromatic scales:** Already-approved five simultaneous 50% resistances and one upgraded Warding; **level 5** remains the leading unapproved minimum-level candidate.
- **Legendary wings:** **Approved** glide levels **1–4**, limited powered flight **5–10**, and sustained flight **11+**, also adopted by Platinum.
- **Champion resilience:** Proposed native fear-source immunity and one failed-save-to-success per Long Rest from level 9, same as the earlier candidate; **not yet approved**.
- **Chromatic Flare and Wrath:** **Approved** automatic death-throes Flare from **level 1**, dealing **1d10 / 2d10 / 3d10 / 4d10** at levels **1 / 5 / 11 / 17**. At **level 11**, Wrath also restores HP to **twice total character level**, and the Flare can resolve **immediately without an Action or be delayed up to one minute for an Action release**. The entire sequence shares **one use per Long Rest**, with no proactive Flare.

**Approval boundary:** The adjusted Wings and specified Wrath/Flare package are **approved alongside Fivefold's prior core**. Breath damage dice, Warding unlock level, fear immunity, and level-9 failed-save intervention remain **unapproved**. Native exceptional content and runtime grants still await Pillar 2 integration.

## Earlier numerical proposal — historical research; superseded for Wings and Wrath/Flare

**Proposal prepared 2026-11-11:** The already approved core remains unchanged: one Breath Weapon with **one** chosen Acid/Cold/Fire/Lightning/Poison damage type and **one** complete 60-foot-by-5-foot line or 30-foot cone per use; simultaneous 50% resistance to all five; one Warding activation that increases all five to 100% for one minute. The following **numbers and additional resilience features require express owner acceptance**. They are not inferred approvals from the existing Platinum design.

### Proposed Breath Weapon progression

| Current total character level | Damage per affected creature (failed save) | Save | Uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1–4** | **2d10** of the chosen Chromatic type | Dexterity, DC **8 + PB + Constitution modifier**, half on success | PB per Long Rest |
| **5–10** | **4d10** | Same | Same |
| **11–16** | **6d10** | Same | Same |
| **17+** | **8d10** | Same | Same |

**Why these numbers:** The damage dice match Platinum's exceptional Heart breath but lack its substitute healing. Fivefold instead has **five damage-type choices and five simultaneous resistances** plus a notably stronger defensive Warding. Doubling the ordinary Dragonborn 1d10/2d10/3d10/4d10 progression preserves the same level bands and is more coherent than assigning damage from an unrelated CR-7 champion's 8d8 necrotic breath or a CR-30 sovereign monster. Choose exactly one type and exactly one area **per use**, independently; no split-damage mode, double area, bonus attacks or increased resource budget. Attack replacement remains **one attack during the Attack action**.

**Balance caution:** Platinum's breath may heal up to three creatures in the chosen area **in place of damage**, while Fivefold's breath only damages. Fivefold's sustained five-type resistance and temporary five-type immunity are a different, strongly encounter-dependent advantage, not an assertion of equal numeric power across all encounters. Reassess through playtesting rather than adding unrequested extra damage or control effects.

### Proposed enhanced Warding level

Set **character level 5** as the unlock for the **already approved** enhanced Chromatic Warding. This preserves the native ordinary Chromatic Warding level threshold, using the already approved **one Action, once per Long Rest, one-minute** duration, but upgrades **all five resistances from 50% to 100% simultaneously** rather than the ordinary leaf's one. An active immunity to one damage type does not confer immunity to associated conditions or effects. The resource is one use **for all five types together**.

**Tradeoff:** Five-type full immunity at level 5 can negate some encounters almost completely; the Action cost and once-per-Long-Rest limit matter but do **not** make this comparable to ordinary Warding. Fivefold is intended to be an exceptional narrative transformation and intentionally strong. It is not subject to ordinary starting-ancestry parity.

### Proposed bounded champion resilience

The published *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* **Dragonborn of Tiamat** has Frightened immunity, **one failed-save-to-success use/day**, and a **separate 8d8 necrotic / Wisdom-save fear breath**. The *Aspect of Tiamat* instead models the five selectable elemental damage types. These are distinct published source models, not a license to combine all their attacks.

- **At character level 1**, grant **immunity to fear-originated effects**, covering sources of native Shaken, Frightened, Panicked and fear-derived Cowering, **but not** independently imposed Cowering or other effects with a nonfear cause. This matches the native `rule.fear-family` decomposition rather than assuming its four conditions are synonyms.
- **At character level 9**, grant **one failed saving throw per Long Rest that may instead succeed**, declared after the failed result without requiring a separate Action. This matches the bounded Platinum-style intervention and is a proposed scaling adaptation of the champion's 1/day Legendary Resistance, not an import of monster Legendary Actions.
- **Do not add an offensive fear rider** to Fivefold's approved one-type elemental Breath Weapon. The combination of selectable damage types, whole-area shape choice and five-type defense is its distinguishing ancestry package. Any later Dread or fear ability should require a separate approved source/feature path, not be implicit.
- **Do not grant** magical sleep/paralysis immunities, Platinum's sensory improvements, innate flight, the champion's necrotic greataxe, or full Aspect abilities to Fivefold through this proposal. Those belong to the Platinum identity, another independent power source, or a separate later decision.

**Reasoning:** All three published Dragonborn champion NPCs share frightened immunity and limited failed-save protection, making them plausible **exceptional champion defensive motifs**. However, owner approval of Platinum's Mind package does **not** by itself authorize them for Fivefold or Ruby. The level-9 gate is a deliberate character-level proposal, consistent with Platinum while preserving strong offensive and defensive differences.

### Late rebirth and open engine integration

Upon a successful sovereign-participated Rite, Fivefold receives its **currently eligible** benefits at the character's **current total character level**. For example, a level-12 reborn character would immediately have a **6d10** chosen-element breath, two authored area choices, all five 50% resistances, level-5 enhanced Warding, fear-source immunity and one failed-save protection per Long Rest **if the above proposal is approved**. Do not restart ancestry progression at level 1, grant five ordinary family resource copies or reset all old character resources automatically. The Rite's **generalized spent-resource carryover and replacement** is still an implementation question across all exceptional forms, not a special Fivefold rule.

**Approval boundary:** This section is a **review-ready candidate**, not finalized selected-native mechanics. Previously approved Fivefold features and geometry remain final; numeric dice, level-5 Warding gate, fear-source immunity and level-9 failed-save protection require their own approval. Ruby and broader category expansion remain outside this scope.

### Comparative balance at each progression point

- **Levels 1–4:** Fivefold has five 50% resistances, damage-type versatility, the proposed 2d10 breath and one automatic 1d10 near-defeat Flare. Platinum has 2d10 breath **plus selective healing** and much broader Mind condition/sense benefits. The proposed fear immunity partially parallels Platinum without importing its sleep/paralysis immunity or Darkvision.
- **Level 5:** Both gain the approved temporary powered flight stage; Fivefold's proposed enhanced Warding upgrades five types for one minute (Action, once per Long Rest) whereas Platinum gains broader healing at 4d10/2d10. **Fivefold can dominate an encounter whose threats rely on those five damage types**; this is exceptional-ancestry power, not ordinary balance.
- **Level 9:** A once-per-Long-Rest failed-save intervention would be shared with Platinum. This is a general champion defense, but Fivefold still lacks Platinum's progressive senses and other Mind immunities. At this level Fivefold also has its 2d10 death-throes Flare.
- **Level 11+:** Both have continuous flight. Fivefold's already approved **Chromatic Wrath** now prevents one otherwise qualifying zero-HP collapse, restores **2 × total level HP**, and lets the player immediately release or delay a **3d10/4d10 Flare**. Platinum instead heals through its breath and continues to improve its senses. Fivefold emphasizes survival and retaliation; Platinum retains sustained support/perception advantages.

### Source distinctions and restraint

*Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* (2021), **Aspect of Tiamat** (p. 166) supports five independent elemental affinities, fivefold immunity, flight, a mythic near-death return and Flare; **Dragonborn of Tiamat** (p. 185) separately provides frightened immunity, one failed-save success per day and a **necrotic fear breath**. The **Aspect has broader condition immunities** than the champion, but **none are automatically granted** to the Fivefold ancestry. Existing owner-approved Dragonborn design already adapts the sovereign features; the remaining champion-style defenses can be shared with Platinum without merging the champion's otherwise incompatible offensive breath.

**Why no further powers:** Additional damage dice, free proactive Flare, monster-scale condition immunities or multiple independent breaths would increase complexity and dilute the established Flare-first last-stand character. The proposed package is intentionally powerful and encounter-dependent; it is **not a claim of numerical parity** with Platinum under every circumstance.

**Approval boundary:** The five rows are a **single coherent recommended final set**, but each is an outstanding owner decision. Do **not** promote them to selected-native, update the Rite as approved, or infer a category-wide legendary chassis from this study. Once the user approves or modifies these values, update the authoritative Fivefold section and the Rite/summary docs, leaving Ruby's distinct sovereign design for its next pass.
