# Pillar 2 — Ruby Dragonborn: exceptional Gem ancestry research

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** **Initial source audit and design candidates only; no mechanics approved.** Ordinary Gem family infrastructure is now selected-native. Do not promote exceptional Ruby to native gameplay or standard mixed-Heritage selection before an independent mechanical pass.

## Approved identity, boundary and ordinary Gem foundation

Ruby is a **singular exceptional Gem sovereign ancestry**, not a composite of Amethyst, Crystal, Emerald, Sapphire and Topaz. Acquiring it ordinarily requires the corresponding Gem category sovereign's **direct personal participation in exceptional Draconic Rebirth**, or explicit DM authorization. The Rite replaces the former current Species and Ancestry package without rewriting the character's historical parents, Background, Upbringing or separately earned class features.

The selected-native **ordinary Gem** parent presently supplies one **15-foot cone** damaging breath bound to the leaf's **Force/Radiant/Psychic/Thunder/Necrotic** type, 50% linked resistance, **30-foot one-way Psionic Mind**, and **level-5 one-minute hovering Gem Flight once per Long Rest**. It also inherits the base Dragonborn Species' Dragon creature type and general Breath Weapon action/resource/damage progression.

**Proposed content model:** Ruby should be its **own exceptional leaf** with explicit trait override/replacement semantics, **not** a sixth default ordinary Gem leaf with all its standard family values automatically copied, and not a recipient of all five Gem affinities. Its own damage type, breath/defense/flight expression and relationship to Psionic Mind must be authored.

## Initial Ruby direction for owner evaluation (not approved)

**Distinct identity:** Prefer **psionic control and defensive projection** coupled with an **exceptional heat-based breath**, rather than a five-type Gem composite. This preserves the distinction between ordinary Gem Dragonborn, Ruby, and Fivefold Chromatic.

- **Breath:** A **single** source-inspired heat breath is the leading candidate; decide whether it deals **Fire** and heats metal like the 5e champion, whether heat is a damage rider on some other authored type, and how it replaces the ordinary Gem cone. Do **not** treat the NPC's Recharge 6 or 6d8 as automatically suitable for a level-1-to-20 player progression.
- **Mind:** Begin with family **Psionic Mind**, then consider an advanced two-way or expanded-range telepathic capacity, psychic resistance, and/or unarmored Intelligence-based defense. A Mind Blade-style projection and psionic spells are **candidates**, not yet categorized as ancestry versus Patron/feat benefits.
- **Flight:** A stronger sustained **hovering** form can distinguish Ruby from ordinary one-minute Gem Flight. Compare the 5e champion's permanent hover with Platinum's distinct staged flight so the final exceptional ancestry has a meaningful presence without merely copying another category.
- **Resilience:** The champion's Frightened immunity and limited failed-save success can be assessed in an eventual **cross-champion design audit**, but adopting Platinum's exact Mind feature sequence is neither required nor implied.

## Decisions required after Fivefold Chromatic

1. Choose Ruby's **breath affinity and environmental rider** (especially Fire, psychic, and how metal-heating works).
2. Choose whether **Mental Defense** is intrinsic and how it interacts with armor/alternative AC calculations.
3. Resolve inherent versus externally acquired **Mind Blade** and psionic magic.
4. Define **flight/hover progression**, upgraded communication and bounded defensive abilities with **total character level**, accounting for late rebirth.
5. Determine the exceptional resource and ancestry override contract without duplicating standard Gem Breath Weapon/Gem Flight or permitting ordinary mixed-Heritage access.
6. Compare power and complexity against the approved Platinum and proposed Fivefold Chromatic without assuming identical packages.

## Editorial and runtime boundaries

The source NPC's ability scores, alignment, humanoid type, spell list, spells per day, weapon attack count, armor class, equipment and CR do not automatically convert to player ancestry mechanics. In first-party player material, use the **Ruby sovereign** descriptor and independently authored narrative presentation, not the copyrighted character identity. No runtime API, native exceptional content, or ordinary mixed-Heritage eligibility is authorized by this research document.
