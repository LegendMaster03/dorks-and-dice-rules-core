# Pillar 2 — Rites of Rebirth and exceptional Dragonborn

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** Owner-approved conceptual replacement rules and Dragonborn content direction. Ritual procedures, exact exceptional traits, data contracts, runtime behavior, and balance are not yet designed or implemented.

## Rite of Rebirth — general transformation rule

A **Rite of Rebirth** is a ritual that **completely replaces a creature's current Species and Ancestry configuration** with a new Species and Ancestry configuration. **Draconic Rebirth** is one specialization of this general class of rituals, not a separate character-creation or inheritance engine.

- **Complete configuration replacement:** The previous Species and Ancestry cease to be the source of the character's *current* inherent mechanical characteristics. All traits, proficiencies, affinities, resistances, intrinsic values, **creature type**, and other benefits granted **by the former Species or Ancestry** are replaced by those granted by the new configuration. A character does not retain an old trait or creature type solely because it once manifested or because a historical parent offered it.
- **Creature type follows the new configuration:** Rebirth replaces the creature type supplied by the previous Species/Ancestry with the creature type defined by the new Species/Ancestry. For example, a Humanoid Human reborn as a Red Dragonborn becomes a Dragon, not both Humanoid and Dragon. Independently sourced effects on creature type remain governed by their own rules; rebirth does not automatically erase effects unrelated to the former Species/Ancestry.
- **No accumulated Species/Ancestry packages:** Rebirth is not trait addition, cross-Species inheritance, or a mixed-Heritage candidate merge. A subsequent Rite replaces the then-current configuration as a whole, even if one Species label happens to remain the same. It must not collect earlier configurations' grants.
- **Independent grants remain independent:** A proficiency or benefit that still exists through an unrelated source (for example, Background, Upbringing, class, or training) is not removed merely because the old Species also granted a similar benefit. Attribution of a grant matters.
- **History does not change:** Biological parents and other historical contributors remain who they were. The original Species/Ancestry and the rite itself belong in the character's history, but historical records do **not** automatically restore old trait eligibility or alter the new manifestation.
- **Life experience does not reset:** Background and Upbringing remain unchanged. The character's origination and prior experiences are not rewritten by the physical or supernatural transformation.
- **Source-neutral framework:** Different rites specify their own target configuration, eligibility, participants, materials, prerequisites and procedure. No universal ritual requirements, durations, costs, power budget, or repeatability restrictions have yet been approved.

**Boundary:** A change that modifies only one trait or separately adjusts only Species *or* Ancestry is not the complete replacement defined here. Other transformation mechanics can be designed independently; this Rite of Rebirth framework must not silently treat partial changes as full rebirths.

### Illustrative outcomes (not new native records)

| Previous configuration | Specialized rite and source | New current Species/Ancestry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Draconic Rebirth involving a Red Dragon | Dragonborn / Red |
| Dragonborn / Red | Draconic Rebirth involving a Gold Dragon | Dragonborn / Gold, replacing Red traits |
| Any mixed Species/Ancestry configuration | Draconic Rebirth involving a Blue Dragon | Dragonborn / Blue; previous contributions remain historical |
| Dragonborn / Gold | Proposed exceptional Draconic Rebirth involving the sovereign of Metallic Dragons | Dragonborn / exceptional Platinum |

These examples establish the **replacement behavior**, not the right of every named source to perform a ritual, an automatic grant, or finalized ritual procedures. The proposed dragon-to-result mapping extends the inspiration from *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons*' Draconic Rebirth gift and the earlier *Races of the Dragon* Rite of Rebirth; the Dorks & Dice general framework is an adaptation, not a claim that the published options are identical.

## Dragonborn exceptional Ancestries

The [three-factor formula](pillar2-dragonborn-expansion-dimensions-20261111.md) remains the content pattern for **ordinary** Dragonborn Ancestries: category, category-related skin material, and damage type. It does not require extraordinary category-leader Ancestries to follow the same pattern.

**Owner direction:** Exceptional forms are not normally available at initial character creation without DM approval. Their intended ordinary in-game acquisition path is an appropriate **Draconic Rebirth** rite requiring the **direct, personal participation of the corresponding category leader**. For the three current exceptional concepts, this means direct participation by the respective Chromatic, Metallic, or Gem category leader. Participation by another powerful dragon, an intermediary, or an artifact is not an automatic substitute. Future exceptional forms must author their own corresponding leader rather than relying on an unrestricted powerful-dragon threshold.

### Intended uses and ordinary-Heritage boundary (owner direction, 2026-11-11)

The reason these exceptional Ancestries are **not ordinarily selectable during initial character creation** is not only their intentionally greater power: exposing them as ordinary Species/Ancestry options could **undermine the mixed-Species and mixed-Ancestry design**, permitting normal candidate selection to assemble extraordinary leader-specific powers without the transformation that defines them.

**Default selection expectation, not a hard narrative prohibition:** In ordinary initial creation and ordinary mixed-Heritage assembly, exceptional category-leader Ancestries and their extraordinary traits are **not automatically eligible contributors or selectable choices**. That does **not** rewrite the character's biological history or prohibit exceptional Dragonborn from having descendants. Exactly what an exceptional parent might contribute to a later character's ordinary inheritance remains a **separate, unapproved design question**; do not silently authorize either the entire exceptional trait package or a universal absolute ban. A DM can expressly authorize extraordinary character origins or make other campaign-specific exceptions.

**Two primary intended story uses, neither an exhaustive restriction on DM authority:**

1. **Major campaign plot:** A narrative directly involving the corresponding extraordinary category leader leads to an exceptional Rite of Rebirth. The rite is part of an event or relationship with that leader rather than ordinary ancestry construction.
2. **Draconic Warlock bargain:** A pact **with the corresponding leader** (for Platinum, the Metallic sovereign; internal research reference: Bahamut) may include Draconic Rebirth among its negotiated terms. Having a Draconic Patron does **not automatically** grant rebirth; the terms and the leader's **direct personal participation** still matter. The Rite is usable outside Warlock, and the Patron is not contingent upon the character already being reborn.

The DM remains free to use these forms differently. Do not implement a two-option whitelist, forbid all exceptional ancestry parentage, or impose universal pact terms solely from these narrative examples.

**Pillar 2 acceptance implications:** (a) Ordinary mixed-Heritage candidate enumeration must not accidentally expose a leader's full exceptional Ancestry through its usual parent/trait inputs. (b) An expressly DM-authorized exceptional origin or a validated exceptional Rite may manifest that Ancestry as a **complete current Species/Ancestry replacement**. (c) Historical parentage and ancestry provenance remain independently recorded. (d) Whether any exceptional aspect can be inherited in a nonexceptional descendant requires an explicitly authored rule, not implicit all-or-nothing inheritance. No executable data/schema behavior is approved by this documentation.

**Power intent:** Exceptional Dragonborn are intentionally and substantially more powerful than ordinary starting Dragonborn Ancestries. Equal mechanical strength with ordinary Ancestries is **not** a balancing objective. Restricted initial selection and the exceptional Rite are acquisition gates, not claims of mechanical parity. When designing these forms, consider the expected campaign power tier, party progression and playtest evidence without using ordinary character-creation balance as the required ceiling. **Platinum's** mechanical features and numerical progression have been approved; **Fivefold Chromatic, Ruby, and other future forms** remain open.

Two structural patterns—composite and singular—are supported across three current exceptional concepts:

1. **Composite leader — Chromatic:** The exceptional Chromatic form represents the sovereign of the five founding Chromatic houses (Black, Blue, Green, Red, White). An extraordinary form may combine characteristics of those **defined founding houses**, rather than recomputing its source list whenever new Chromatic Ancestries are published. The **core manifestations** are approved: choose one of five damage types and one of two whole breath areas per activation, maintain 50% resistance to all five types, and enhance all five with one Warding activation. **Damage dice, Warding unlock level, and supplemental champion abilities remain unresolved**. Composite does **not** mean an automatic union of all five complete trait packages.
2. **Singular leader — Metallic:** The Platinum form represents the singular sovereign of Metallic Dragons. An extraordinary Platinum Ancestry is a **distinct singular material/type**, not a composite of Copper, Bronze, Brass, Silver, and Gold. Its **Platinum feature package is approved** in the separate design specification; the exceptional ancestry is not yet an executable native record and does not gain an automatic copy of ordinary Metallic features.
3. **Singular leader — Gem:** Ruby Dragonborn represents the exceptional sovereign of Gem Dragons, as a singular Ruby identity rather than an automatic combination of all ordinary Gem Ancestries. Its [initial source and mechanics study](pillar2-ruby-dragonborn-concept-20261111.md) compares psionic defenses, heat breath, mental weaponry and sustained hover with the now selected-native ordinary Gem family. These are **unapproved candidates**, not a playable record or approved feature specification.

### Platinum — Heart, Mind, and Wings (approved design; expanded area choice)

Platinum is a **singular exceptional Ancestry** representing the Metallic sovereign, not a composite of standard Metallic leaves. **The Platinum mechanical design is owner-approved (2026-11-11)** and is recorded in the [authoritative Heart, Mind, and Wings specification](pillar2-platinum-heart-mind-wings-concept-20261111.md). All three expressions manifest together when acquired through exceptional Draconic Rebirth. Its existing Species-derived creature type is **Dragon**, and the lineage is modeled as a distinct Dragonborn leaf that **replaces** ordinary damaging Breath Weapon rather than inheriting the separate ordinary Metallic Enervating/Repulsion Breath.

- **Heart:** One combined Breath Weapon, choosing **one 60-foot-by-5-foot line or 30-foot cone per use**, replacing one attack in the Attack action, **PB uses per Long Rest**. It deals **radiant damage** to unselected creatures (Dexterity save DC **8 + PB + Constitution modifier**, half on success) and instead heals **up to three chosen other creatures** in the area. Both outcomes occur within one use, and no chosen healing recipient also takes damage from that breath. Across character levels **1–4 / 5–10 / 11–16 / 17+**, damage is **2d10 / 4d10 / 6d10 / 8d10** and healing per recipient is **1d10 / 2d10 / 3d10 / 4d10**. Healing respects maximum HP and does not revive the dead or cure conditions. **50% radiant resistance** is inherent.
- **Mind:** The Darkvision progression by total character level is **30 feet at level 1**, **60 at 6**, **90 at 9**, **120 at 12**; **Blindsense 30 feet at level 15**. The approved proposal includes immunity to **fear-originated effects** (respecting the native distinct fear conditions), **Paralyzed**, and magical sleep, plus **one failed saving throw changed to success per Long Rest beginning at character level 9**. Specific native condition/effect references must be respected when integrating.
- **Wings:** Gliding at **levels 1–4** (up to **30 feet horizontally per turn**, descending **5 feet for every 20 feet horizontally**, with safe controlled landings), **30-foot powered flight at levels 5–10** for **one minute once per Short or Long Rest**, and **unrestricted-duration 30-foot flight at level 11+**. No hover capability is implied; standard movement restrictions apply.
- **Late rebirth:** Resolve the **entire Platinum progression using current total character level** at the instant the Rite takes effect; a level-15 character acquires the level-15 Platinum capabilities immediately. No separate ancestry-level progression, stacked former Species traits, or automatic Warlock Patron grant is involved.

**Boundary:** This is an approved **design** and does not mean native gameplay content, an executable Rite, or general mixed-Heritage eligibility are implemented. Platinum is not an ordinary initial-creation/mixed-ancestry selection without DM authorization. Open **general implementation** matters include the exact engine representation of magical sleep, Blindsense/condition interactions, involuntary falling while incapacitated, and how limited-use resources carry over through successive rebirths. An optional **1d6 involuntary-fall damage cap** and a proposed spent-resource carryover formula are not approved Platinum grants. No additional Platinum feature-design pass is required before integration; future playtesting may motivate adjustments.

**Approved shared flight timing (2026-11-11):** Platinum and Fivefold both glide at **levels 1–4**, fly **30 feet for one minute once per Short or Long Rest at levels 5–10**, and fly **30 feet continuously from level 11+**, without hover. This supersedes Platinum's historical 1–5 / 6–11 / 12+ progression. Fivefold's **level-11 Chromatic Wrath** and one linked **Chromatic Flare** are also approved, without importing the Aspect's monster-scale mythic phase. See the [Fivefold design](pillar2-fivefold-chromatic-concept-20261111.md) and [Platinum specification](pillar2-platinum-heart-mind-wings-concept-20261111.md).

### Fivefold Chromatic — approved core, Wings and Wrath/Flare; numerical details pending

The [dedicated Fivefold Chromatic specification](pillar2-fivefold-chromatic-concept-20261111.md) records the **owner-approved core direction (2026-11-11)**. Fivefold is one exceptional ancestry bound to the Black, Blue, Green, Red and White founding Chromatic houses, not five complete ordinary Dragonborn packages.

- **Breath:** One Breath Weapon, independently choosing **one Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning or Poison** damage type and **one 60-foot-by-5-foot line or 30-foot cone** on **each use**. A use applies one type to one chosen area; it does not create five simultaneous effects, both areas, or separate resource pools. Retain the normal Attack-action replacement and **PB uses per Long Rest** pending final damage-dice decisions.
- **Resistances:** **Simultaneous 50% resistance** to all five founding damage types, replacing ordinary single-affinity resistance without stacking repeated applications.
- **Warding:** One enhanced Chromatic Warding feature, **one Action, once per Long Rest**, raising the five resistances **together** to **100% for one minute**, replacing rather than adding percentages. The exact unlock level is pending.
- **Wings:** Approved gliding at **1–4**, limited **30-foot** flight at **5–10** for one minute once per Short or Long Rest, and sustained **30-foot** flight at **11+**, without hover.
- **Chromatic Wrath (level 11):** Once per Long Rest, when damage would reduce the character to 0 HP without killing them outright, they remain conscious with HP equal to **twice total character level**. This does not reset other ancestry resources.
- **Chromatic Flare (linked to Wrath):** Once in the minute after Wrath, use one Action for a **15-foot-radius burst centered on the character**, selecting affected creatures and **one of the five Chromatic damage types**. Dexterity save against **8 + PB + Constitution modifier** for half of **3d10 at levels 11–16** or **4d10 at 17+**. It spends no Breath Weapon charge and grants no off-turn Action, proactive use, or extra charge.
- **Not yet selected:** Breath damage dice, Warding unlock threshold, offensive fear rider, fear-source immunity, and limited failed-save protection. The published champion's necrotic fear breath is separate from the approved elemental breath.
- **Acquisition:** The exceptional Rite requires the Chromatic sovereign's direct personal participation (or express DM authorization), completely replaces the former Species/Ancestry package and does not expose extraordinary traits through ordinary mixed-Heritage selection.

**Historical design reference for later Platinum work:** The existing original Dragonborn notes at `data/rulesets/first-party-001/working/species/subspecies/dragonborn/SHARED_BREATH_REFERENCE.md` explicitly discuss *Dragonborn of Bahamut* from *Races of the Dragon* (3.5), including the Heart aspect's round-based Breath Weapon recharge. That 3.5 transformation also offers **Heart, Mind, or Wings** as an aspect choice with distinct advancement, a useful design precedent for exceptional ancestral manifestations, **not adopted Dorks & Dice mechanics**. The same legacy notes separately defer **Dragonfire Adept-derived feat/prestige-class** concepts; Dragonfire Adept is not itself the Dragonborn of Bahamut ancestry or its transformation. Another historical Bahamut-linked prestige class is **Vassal of Bahamut** (*Book of Exalted Deeds*); its mechanics have not been selected for this ancestry. In particular, the original 3.5 transformed race's partial retention of prior racial characteristics and Humanoid (dragonblood) type must **not** silently override our separately established **complete Species/Ancestry and creature type replacement**.

These three descriptive identities—**Fivefold Chromatic**, **Platinum**, and **Ruby**—are the current working exceptional Dragonborn concepts. Other categories may later use composite, singular, or different patterns, or have no exceptional Ancestry.

### Identity and publication boundaries

**Owner-approved editorial direction:** In player-facing first-party material, identify these extraordinary dragons by their **category-leader roles and distinctive Ancestry descriptions**, not by the D&D-specific characters used as research inspirations. Use the descriptive Chromatic sovereign, Platinum sovereign, and Ruby sovereign concepts; do not require a coined proper name for any of them. A reader may recognize the inspiration, but the published material should not rely on recognizing any external character.

**Research-only references:** Tiamat, Bahamut, and Sardior can remain named in internal source and design comparisons. These names are not the first-party identities of the proposed leaders. Descriptions, mythology, visual portrayal, relationships, and lore in any eventual player-facing text should be independently authored; **removing a character's name alone does not eliminate concerns about copying distinctive protected expression**. The precise final narrative descriptions remain open. This is an editorial design boundary, not a legal clearance.

**Separation from ordinary mixed Heritage:** The already approved ordinary Breath Weapon rule permits independent selection of a contributed damage type and **complete** area. That rule continues to apply to eligible ordinary mixed-Heritage candidates; it does not give a player access to an exceptional Ancestry, merge five separate Breath Weapons, or resolve exceptional abilities.

## Pillar 2 representation consequences (design requirements, not finalized schema)

1. Track **historical origination/contributors** separately from **current Species/Ancestry** and their manifested characteristic provenance. A rebirth can change the latter without modifying the former.
2. The prospective Rite operation needs explicit **complete replacement semantics** and source-qualified removal/retention of grants, not the general inherited-candidate pool's merge procedure.
3. Make **ordinary versus exceptional** eligibility explicit in authored content; material names or compatible damage types alone must not accidentally unlock ritual-only options through ordinary creation or mixing.
4. Preserve the specific exceptional form's authored identity and constituents; do not derive a composite's membership dynamically from every new category leaf.
5. Reuse source-neutral characteristic definitions and rules where appropriate. No dedicated Dragonborn-only Origin engine is needed, but ritual eligibility and transformation must be general-purpose concepts.
6. Neither the present read-only Pillar 2 profiles nor the existing native catalogs currently execute rebirth, remove grants, or enforce ritual-gated character creation.

## Unresolved design decisions

- General Rite of Rebirth procedure, costs, timing, failure conditions and reversibility; the exceptional Draconic Rebirth requirement for direct category-leader participation is established, but the remaining details of that participation are not.
- Exact traits, damage choices, and resistances for **Fivefold Chromatic, Ruby and future exceptional forms**. Platinum's designed features are approved, while native implementation and playtest-driven revisions remain separate.
- Final persistent schema and rules-engine representation for successive rebirths and their historical record.
- Whether more Dragon categories receive leader-associated forms and how those are acquired.

**Scope:** Design record only. No first-party Species/Ancestry content changes, character creation permissions, runtime APIs, combat rules, or ritual execution are authorized by this document.
