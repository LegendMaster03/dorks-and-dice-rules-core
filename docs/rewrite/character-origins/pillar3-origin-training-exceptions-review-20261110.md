## Question being evaluated

The **default for an Origin is no Competency proficiency and no Competency rank**. Only a real historical source benefit being converted warrants the starting conversion heuristic (Upbringing: proficiency; Background: +1 rank). A narrower or stronger allocation may be justified by the source's actual training and the complete Origin's relative value. The rule is **not** a requirement to add Competencies to every Background or Upbringing.

Rank grants are substantial because they affect established advancement, crafting efficiency, and access to rank-based abilities/traits, in addition to checks. This review does not redefine those mechanics or estimate a rank's power as a flat +1 bonus.

Species defaults are **suggestions**, not simultaneous extra Upbringings. A culturally chosen Upbringing is not restricted to one Species unless a separate actual rule says so.

The comparisons above deliberately isolate the cultural substitution while retaining those inherent traits on both sides.

## Recommended review order and decision boundaries

1. **First:** Establish source-specific amounts for the five genuinely redesigned native Competency choice sites (Explorer, Spy, Aristocratic, General Goliath, Street-Raised). There is no global grant implied while these remain unallocated. The selected alternative itself remains approved.
2. **Then:** Test the General Dwarf dual-benefit possibility against those settled comparators, especially a Dwarf with a cross-cultural General Goliath or Wilderness-Raised Upbringing. An extra rank can unlock abilities and should not be awarded solely to equalize displayed benefits.
3. **Then:** Reconsider Guild Artisan and Charlatan only if actual package/playtest evidence supports divergence from the historical conversion guideline. Rock Gnome is a lower-priority dual-benefit candidate because its whole package already has substantial additional features.
4. **Throughout:** Direct binary Thieves' Tools conversions remain settled in Criminal, Spy and Street-Raised; unselected options confer nothing. Folk Hero/Hermit intentional omissions remain settled unless explicitly reopened.

**No new grants or allocations are authorized by this document.** Existing native JSON and previously approved selection counts are unaffected. The next substantive act is a source-specific review of the five unallocated native choice sites, not a generic pass that grants every Background a rank or every Upbringing proficiency.
