Provenance includes source record pointer, exact PDF hash and actual PDF page numbers (not the source pack's unrelated book page numbers).

- SRD 5.1 skills: pages 78–83; conditions: pages 358–359.
- SRD 5.2.1 skills: page 9; conditions: pages 177–191 (exact page recorded per condition).
- SRD 5.1 Exhaustion: the pinned unofficial condition includes an extra resurrection-based recovery clause absent from the publisher's CC PDF at page 358. Removed only in the edition-specific native definition; the source corpus stays unchanged.
- SRD 5.2.1 conditions preserve their changed behavior, including the Grappled attack penalty, Invisible initiative benefit, Petrified condition immunity and Stunned not independently setting Speed to 0. Do not merge these with the 2014 rules or first-party mechanics.

The records link to local source-page IDs and known local condition references. 5e.tools markup is preserved as descriptive source formatting; it is not a guarantee of full tag resolution or executable mechanics. The remaining imported candidate categories still require structured promotion and fidelity review. No first-party definition changed.
