# Fighter — Band 1 reconciliation (partial; attack progression deferred)

**Status:** Partially approved design, October 23, 2026; not implemented as a native class. Fighter class levels 1–20 only. No subclasses, prestige classes, Band 2, or runtime.

## Approved by owner

1. **Historical Fighter bonus feats:** Grant the eleven historical combat-feat selections at Fighter levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, and 20, alongside (not instead of) modern advancement grants. Preserve actual combat-feat eligibility and prerequisites; do not duplicate equivalent benefits.
2. **Action Surge:** Use 2024 procedure: one additional eligible Action on the turn, **excluding the Magic action**. One use at Fighter level 2, two from level 17, at most one per turn; recover after Short or Long Rest.
3. **Second Wind:** Use 2024 pool: 2 uses at Fighter levels 1–3; 3 at 4–9; 4 at 10–20. Bonus Action restores 1d10 + Fighter class level HP. Recover one use on Short Rest and all on Long Rest. Tactical Mind consumes this same resource.
4. **Existing general rules:** No permanent universal ability-score maximum. Fighter 19 awards an ordinary ASI or otherwise eligible feat, while Epic Boons require total character level 21. No subclass features are authorized here.

The comparison also identifies compatible Fighter features and historical abilities (Fighting Style, Weapon Mastery, Tactical Mind, Tactical Shift, Tactical Master, Studied Attacks, Indomitable, proficiencies, ranked skills). They remain to be finalized as native declarative definitions; recording them here is not a gameplay-implementation claim.

## Unresolved: attack progression and BAB

The owner rejected keeping 5.x Extra Attack almost unchanged with a special movement restriction during Fighter levels 16–19. That does not constitute a meaningful reconciliation.

Historical BAB iterative attacks and modern Extra Attack are expressions of the **same underlying capability**. The intended resolution is one native Attack action and underlying attack-resolution system, with an approachable ordinary sequence and appropriately granted advanced options. Potential additional depth must provide real capability, retain eligibility/costs, and avoid inventing duplicate attacks by summing edition-specific schedules.

Exact native attack counts, accuracy arithmetic, full-attack commitments, movement restrictions, and multiclass contributions **remain undecided**. They should be considered independently as common combat-system fundamentals rather than forced into a Fighter-only ruling. Do not treat a proposed attack progression as approved.

The historical and modern saving throw/ranked-skill inputs likewise remain subject to already established general-system conversion; do not turn these into new Fighter-specific blockers.

## Workflow

Owner approved deferring Fighter's final reconciliation and proceeding next with a four-edition **Rogue** base-class comparison. Retain the Fighter pending-native-reconciliation candidate file; do not mark Fighter complete or deploy a new runtime. Further design review and implementation remain separate steps.
