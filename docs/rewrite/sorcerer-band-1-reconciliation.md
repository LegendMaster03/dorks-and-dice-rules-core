# Sorcerer — Band 1 source comparison and decisions for review

**Status (2026-10-23):** Sorcerer Metamagic design is **approved**, including the class-specific option system, names, costs, eligibility, Twinned Spell, combinations, 2024 option-learning progression and Arcane Apotheosis discount. **Sorcerous Restoration's additive level-20 reconciliation is now owner-approved.** Default finite spells-known progression and the cross-class familiar/companion choice remain for review; the owner is still considering how to define bonded familiars. Innate Sorcery/Sorcery Incarnate carry their 2024 source rules; Flexible Casting is shared compatibility testing, not another class-design question. No native Sorcerer class implementation has been made. The owner approved the class review sequence **Sorcerer → Wizard → Monk** after Rogue. Restrict this pass to Sorcerer class levels 1–20. Subclasses/origins/bloodlines, prestige classes, higher level bands, and executable spell/point conversion are outside this class pass.

## Already established across Dorks & Dice

- **One native spellcasting system, independent of class-specific pools:** Players may choose the class-eligible **spell slots or spell points** resource profile. **Sorcery Points are a distinct Sorcerer class resource in both profiles**, used for Metamagic and other explicitly granted Sorcerer features. They do not become the casting spell-point pool, and choosing spell-point casting does not merge or replace Sorcery Points. The separate-resource distinction is already established, not a new owner decision. Only explicit feature-based conversion can transfer value between the casting pool and Sorcery Points.
- **No daily spell preparation; optional Open Spell Access:** Daily selection of a prepared spell subset is removed in the native rules. Independently, **Open Spell Access** is an owner-reaffirmed optional GM/campaign rule that removes **spells-known selection caps**, allowing all class-list spells of an eligible **class and accessible spell level** (including cantrips). Its activation does **not** increase available slots, casting spell points, Sorcery Points, maximum spell level, or class-feature resources. Without this option, Bard retains its finite spells-known progression, Cleric and Druid retain their separately approved full-list access, and Sorcerer's ordinary finite default advancement still needs reconciliation. Enabling this spell-access option **does not automatically grant every Metamagic option**.
- **Rest-based recharge** is standard for class resources unless an explicit exception has a compelling calendar-time purpose.
- **Band 1** is class levels 1–20, with Epic Boons available as feats only when total character level reaches 21. No permanent universal ability score maximum is imposed.
- **General combat and shared spell rules**, including action economy, other spellcasting classes, familiar/controlled creature actions, resource exchange and feats, should be centralized rather than customized separately for Sorcerer.

## Remaining Sorcerer work — consolidated audit (2026-10-23)

**Status distinction:** Rules already settled by the owner or fully specified by a nonconflicting source are **not new design blockers**. This audit does not mark the unfinished Sorcerer class as implemented or silently approve source-conflict resolutions.

### Approved additive Sorcerous Restoration

**Owner decision (2026-10-23):** Keep the **2024 Sorcerous Restoration** from level 5. Starting at **Sorcerer level 20**, completing **every Short Rest** recovers an **additional 4 spent Sorcery Points**, including Short Rests when the level-5 feature is also used. At level 20, a Sorcerer who has sufficient points expended may recover **10 + 4 = 14** on the first chosen eligible Short Rest after a Long Rest; later Short Rests restore **4** until the level-5 once-per-Long-Rest benefit becomes available again. All recovery is capped by the standard Sorcery Point maximum (20 at Sorcerer 20). Baseline Font of Magic and Long Rest recovery, spell-slot-to-Sorcery-Point conversion, and the already approved Arcane Apotheosis maximum 2-point-per-turn cost discount remain distinct and unchanged. The additional 4 is **not** an alternative, replacement, or offset against the earlier level-5 recovery.

### Remaining source and shared-framework choices

1. **Finite spells known with Open Spell Access disabled:** The 2024 level-1–20 table provides a straightforward and more generous default progression (22 leveled spells by level 20) than the 2014 15-spell endpoint. Owner indicated this is straightforward; the exact default table is still to be transcribed/confirmed as the class definition. No daily preparation; optional Open Spell Access removes finite selection caps.
2. **Shared familiar / companion access choice:** Owner is considering replacing automatic availability of multiple creature-granting base-class features with a **choice between bonded/persistent and ephemeral/conjured** access, potentially across all eligible base classes. This must be settled at the **grant/choice level** before designing detailed bonded familiar improvements. A 3.5e Sorcerer's **Familiar**, a 3.5e Druid's **Animal Companion**, and a 2024 Druid's **Wild Companion familiar** are **not mechanically the same feature**. Do not convert the Druid's animal companion into a familiar by name alone, or silently supersede the previously approved Druid coexisting-companions decision; the latter remains effective unless the owner explicitly revises it. Specific Bonded Familiar mechanics and penalties are **intentionally deferred pending the owner's further idea**.

### Already settled or directly inherited — no additional design approvals needed

- **Innate Sorcery (level 1):** Carry the 2024 Bonus Action activation, 1-minute duration, two uses per Long Rest, +1 Sorcerer spell DC, and Advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks. No cross-edition conflict exists with an earlier feature.
- **Sorcery Incarnate (level 7):** Carry 2024 activation for 2 Sorcery Points when Innate Sorcery uses are exhausted; the ability to use up to two Metamagic options during active Innate Sorcery follows the already-approved Metamagic rules.
- **Font of Magic:** Retain modern Sorcery Point class progression and independent Long Rest recovery; Sorcery Points do not merge with casting spell points.
- **Flexible Casting / optional casting spell points:** Carry existing 5e class conversion amounts and the established optional spell-point resource variant. Check ambiguous slot-conversion edge cases in the **shared spellcasting implementation**, not as a prerequisite to creating a native Sorcerer class definition. Do not claim all conversion edges are already executable.
- **Metamagic:** Names, costs, option progression, Twinned modes, combination limits and level-20 Arcane Apotheosis discount are explicitly approved in `docs/rewrite/sorcerer-metamagic-cross-edition-comparison.md`; do not reopen.
- **Ordinary class attributes and advancement:** Use modern **d6 Hit Die**, Charisma casting, Constitution/Charisma saves, simple weapon proficiency, full-caster progression, ASIs at levels 4/8/12/16/19, Epic Boons only when total character level reaches 21, and subclass placeholders at 3/6/14/18. Handle shared competency/class-skill conversion through established general rules instead of devising one-off Sorcerer rules.
- **Spell access:** Daily preparation is removed; Open Spell Access is an optional campaign setting. Both are already settled. The separate **Open Metamagic** idea remains a proposal, not an approved native rule and not a class-implementation blocker.
- **Declarative versus executable status:** The native 20-level data and feature declarations can be implemented before unrelated shared engine work on slot conversion and familiars is fully executable, provided pending integrations are accurately marked.

**Implementation sequencing:** Sorcerous Restoration is fully resolved; implement it additively. Apply the straightforward default finite spell progression without reopening preparation or resource systems. **Do not finalize familiar access or detailed Bonded Familiar mechanics** while the owner is considering the cross-class choice; reserve a clearly pending familiar grant if necessary. After its access policy is settled, reconcile the native Sorcerer class, feature records and tests. Do not restart a general cross-edition review on settled details.

## Boundaries

Comparing 3.0/3.5e/2014/2024 is not an automatic latest-edition preference. The owner generally regards 3.5e as an improvement on 3.0e but prefers many 2014 mechanics over 2024, while retaining useful 2024 additions. **This file records approved Metamagic mechanics but remains a proposal and source inventory for the rest of the class**: do not mark the entire Sorcerer base class or source candidate statuses reconciled before its remaining mechanics receive approval. Nothing here reopens already approved Bard, Cleric or Druid spells/slots/points rulings.
