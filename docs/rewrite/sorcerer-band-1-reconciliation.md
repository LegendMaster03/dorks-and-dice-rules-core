# Sorcerer — Band 1 source comparison and decisions for review

**Status (2026-10-23):** Sorcerer Metamagic design is **approved**, including the class-specific option system, names, costs, eligibility, Twinned Spell, combinations, 2024 option-learning progression and Arcane Apotheosis discount. **Remaining non-Metamagic Sorcerer class decisions are still open** and no native Sorcerer class implementation has been made. The owner approved the class review sequence **Sorcerer → Wizard → Monk** after Rogue. Restrict this pass to Sorcerer class levels 1–20. Subclasses/origins/bloodlines, prestige classes, higher level bands, and executable spell/point conversion are outside this class pass.

## Already established across Dorks & Dice

- **One native spellcasting system, independent of class-specific pools:** Players may choose the class-eligible **spell slots or spell points** resource profile. **Sorcery Points are a distinct Sorcerer class resource in both profiles**, used for Metamagic and other explicitly granted Sorcerer features. They do not become the casting spell-point pool, and choosing spell-point casting does not merge or replace Sorcery Points. The separate-resource distinction is already established, not a new owner decision. Only explicit feature-based conversion can transfer value between the casting pool and Sorcery Points.
- **No daily spell preparation; optional Open Spell Access:** Daily selection of a prepared spell subset is removed in the native rules. Independently, **Open Spell Access** is an owner-reaffirmed optional GM/campaign rule that removes **spells-known selection caps**, allowing all class-list spells of an eligible **class and accessible spell level** (including cantrips). Its activation does **not** increase available slots, casting spell points, Sorcery Points, maximum spell level, or class-feature resources. Without this option, Bard retains its finite spells-known progression, Cleric and Druid retain their separately approved full-list access, and Sorcerer's ordinary finite default advancement still needs reconciliation. Enabling this spell-access option **does not automatically grant every Metamagic option**.
- **Rest-based recharge** is standard for class resources unless an explicit exception has a compelling calendar-time purpose.
- **Band 1** is class levels 1–20, with Epic Boons available as feats only when total character level reaches 21. No permanent universal ability score maximum is imposed.
- **General combat and shared spell rules**, including action economy, other spellcasting classes, familiar/controlled creature actions, resource exchange and feats, should be centralized rather than customized separately for Sorcerer.

The comparison covers general feats versus Sorcerer-only options; higher-slot costs versus Sorcery Points; 3.x multi-feat stacking and spontaneous casting-time restrictions; individual historical feat effects; and 2014-to-2024 changes including Twinned Spell. **Owner has approved the detailed native Metamagic rules:** adaptations are **Amplified Spell (historical Empower)**, **Ascendant Spell (historical Heighten)**, **Widened Spell**, and **Maximized Spell**, with the specified costs, spell-level/initial-roll restrictions, Twinned Spell modes, level 2/10/17 option progression, per-spell combinations, and the bounded Arcane Apotheosis discount. ## Remaining Sorcerer Band 1 decisions (Metamagic details approved)

1. **Flexible Casting — shared implementation/testing, not owner-design blocker:** The DMG's 2014 **optional Spell Points variant** is the established casting-resource profile; Sorcery Points remain separately tracked, and the Sorcerer uses the approved Flexible Casting source costs and action timing. Adapt/check exact slot-exchange behavior against the published variant in the **shared spellcasting subsystem**, including temporary-slot conversion and resource limits. The optional variant does not explicitly adjudicate every Flexible Casting case, so do not claim the entire interaction is already executable or introduce unsupported rules. **The Sorcerer native class declaration does not need a new design decision here.**
2. **Default spell-known advancement only:** Open Spell Access already supports full access to every Sorcerer class spell of a castable level as an **optional campaign rule**, and daily preparation is removed in every mode. Decide only the Sorcerer's **normal, option-off** finite spells-known progression, reconciling 3.5e/2014/2024 counts where appropriate. Do not create a separate Sorcerer-only unrestricted-list rule or equate access to all Metamagic options with access to all spells.
3. **Innate Sorcery and Sorcery Incarnate:** Metamagic option names/costs, option-learning progression, Twinned Spell modes, combinations and Arcane Apotheosis's maximum two-point discount are **approved**. Reconcile the remaining non-Metamagic effects of Innate Sorcery and Sorcery Incarnate, their uses, and their fit in the class progression without reopening settled Metamagic details.
4. **Restoration and capstones:** Reconcile level-5 2024 Sorcerous Restoration, level-20 2014 Restoration and 2024 Arcane Apotheosis into one progression without redundant restoration.
5. **Historical Familiar and baseline advancement:** Preserve meaningful companion capability, but revisit XP penalties and extremely long replacement times; finish Hit Die, proficiencies, subclass placeholders, ASIs and the level 1–20 table.

## Boundaries

Comparing 3.0/3.5e/2014/2024 is not an automatic latest-edition preference. The owner generally regards 3.5e as an improvement on 3.0e but prefers many 2014 mechanics over 2024, while retaining useful 2024 additions. **This file records approved Metamagic mechanics but remains a proposal and source inventory for the rest of the class**: do not mark the entire Sorcerer base class or source candidate statuses reconciled before its remaining mechanics receive approval. Nothing here reopens already approved Bard, Cleric or Druid spells/slots/points rulings.
