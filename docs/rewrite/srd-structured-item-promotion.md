# Structured item promotion

The selected equipment source arrays now produce 630 native SRD 5.1 records and 641 native SRD 5.2.1 records. The old package contains 493 concrete items, 91 base items, six item groups, 30 magic-variant templates and ten weapon properties. The modern package contains 465 concrete items (including one publisher-only recovery), 93 base items, 25 item groups, 41 magic-variant templates, nine weapon properties and eight weapon masteries.

Run `node tools/promote_srd_items.mjs --check` to verify the reproducible structured projection. The edition review workflow independently validates the pinned source hashes and the complete local catalog reference graph. The imported payload retains equipment stats, cost/weight, attunement, descriptions, nested tables, spell links, weapon properties, mastery and magic-variant requirements/inheritance where present. Generic templates remain distinct from concrete items; the import does not implement variant materialization or item execution.

Source occurrences are tracked by pointer. Schema-only item types and reusable entry templates are excluded from standalone rule promotion. Top-level supplemental tool and vehicle descriptions are omitted pending verified SRD provenance. The two obsolete modern firearm duplicates and six legacy firearm-property metadata occurrences are excluded. The edition reports record these decisions. Source page numbers are hints, not automatically asserted as verified publisher pages.

Known corrections include the modern Cube of Force spell identities, Mysterious Deck non-SRD inset/Bastion material and table name, Dragon Orb setting/legacy mechanics, modern poison names, both editions' Handy Haversack references, old gaming set members, and old Orb of Dragonkind setting names and foreign creature tag. The old artifact's official random properties and Tiamat references remain. The modern Plate Armor of Etherealness was recovered from publisher page 234; its eligible armor types and use conditions are preserved.

The modern Giant Fly is a separate official stat block embedded in the Figurine of Wondrous Power (p. 222) missing from the pinned bestiary. It is recovered with its published stats and zero XP; no attack is invented. The item's inline reference resolves to the local recovered definition.

The item reports record 724 old and 1,099 modern validated-target reference occurrences across the generated item catalog, including source pages. All 47 previously unresolved monster item tags now resolve to concrete local equipment. Six item markup/structured references remain deliberately unresolved: the old Carrion Crawler mention (no local stat block), two specific magic weapons requiring generic template materialization, and three individual firearm-bullet references where the catalog contains a ten-bullet package but no standalone unit. No cross-edition fallback or invented data is used.

These are selected pinned inventory counts, not certified counts of independent publisher headings; generated concrete variants, body fidelity, templates, descriptive rule tags and broader reference coverage remain under review. No distribution approval or new executable mechanics is implied.

## Canonical vehicle profile reconciliation

Both `vehicles.json` candidates refer to the existing Apparatus of the Crab magic item. The original publisher stat profile and lever table are on SRD 5.1 p.208 and SRD 5.2.1 p.210. The item generator retains one identity (`item.apparatus-of-the-crab`) per package, adds a typed `vehicleProfile` with size, crew/passenger capacity, AC, HP, movement and damage immunities, and records the additional exact vehicle source pointer. The speed restriction retains legs and tail in 5.1 versus legs in 5.2.1. Internal obsolete Kwalish naming is corrected to the official name in both item bodies/tables. Source-added sea-only terrain metadata is not promoted; the apparatus can walk and swim. No duplicate body or executable vehicle mechanics are introduced.
